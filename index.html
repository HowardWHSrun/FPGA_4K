Board comments

Open items per board from design review V3, updated 13 November 2026 against Frontend 14 layers V7, FPGA-LDO A layout V16 and ASIC boards V7. Not fabrication approval.

Whole systemAll boards together · report V4, 14 Nov 2026
Not ready to order

Items that matter (report V4, 14 Nov)

  • R2S-03PCBWay has not confirmed ten layers in 0.8 mm for the ASIC boards.
  • R2S-05Each port's 44 ASIC outputs return through only two ground contacts.
  • SYS-01The cable data rate is not set, and the only recorded rate (1.25 Gb/s) is too slow for eight ASICs.
  • SYS-02No cable is chosen, and a thin cable can push a stack into the 637 mA port limit.
  • SYS-03Head heat is not estimated or limited.
  • SYS-09The connectors are unkeyed, so an ASIC board can mate turned 180°.
  • SYS-31The ASIC designer still owes the die and bond-loop height, the capacitor values and the VDD_CGEN level.
  • SYS-56There is no grounding plan for eight stacks on one animal.
  • SYS-57Panel break-off tabs sit beside mating parts on the Frontend and FPGA-LDO A.
  • SYS-58The enclosure has no room for the XEM8310's heat sink.
  • SYS-60All eight ASICs share the stimulation control lines, and their settings cannot be read back.
  • SYS-61There is no bring-up procedure, and a stacked head has no test points.

HDMI Extension BoardV37 · edited 10 Nov 2026, 13:38 CST
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • HDMI-42JLC can no longer supply MC1/MC2 (BTE-040-01-F-D-A, C2914307: 10 in stock, -8 orderable), so the unpaid 10-piece cart line for five HDMI V37 boards needs a pre-order or Global Sourcing; MC3 (5 already held) and C5 (cart already on C401715) are not new shortages. New
0 blockers1 major27 minors27 notes

New in V3, smaller items

  • HDMI-43The back silkscreen says the red LEDs D701-D708 mean 'port 5 V on', but they light only when a port switch reports a fault. New
  • HDMI-44Y500's load mismatch puts the FT232H clock about 20-55 ppm fast, outside the FT232H's own +/-30 ppm input limit. New
  • HDMI-45The board does not show which setting the JTAG selectors SW600 and SW601 are in, so the target port and the OFF position cannot be read from the board. New
  • HDMI-46U700's output capacitors C704-C707 are X5R parts rated only to 85 C, not the X7R/X6S/X7S types TI lists, and two sit 1.3 mm from the hot module. New
  • HDMI-47One U700 feedback fault can drive FE_5V to about 11.9 V, past the 7 V port switches and the heads' 6.5 V input limit, and nothing on FE_5V clamps it. New
  • HDMI-48A full XCAU25P bitstream takes 0.26-0.49 s to load from the XEM8310 flash, 2.6-4.9 times the PCIe 100 ms boot limit, so only a Tandem PROM build can meet it. New
  • HDMI-49Enclosure V3 powers the DFR1169 and the XEM8310 from the same 12 V with the Thunderbolt cable left in, so the endpoint can miss first link training, and nothing documents whether the Mac finds it later. New
  • HDMI-50With SW601 at ENABLE and SW600 at 0, a connected Vivado Hardware Manager reaches the XEM JTAG 12-28 ms after power-up and can break its 0.26-0.49 s boot from flash. New
  • HDMI-51The XEM8310 constraint files set none of Opal Kelly's flash-boot options, so a default build would take about 46 s to load from flash. New
  • HDMI-52SW600 is rated only to +60 C and the XEM8310 to +70 C, both below the upper estimate of the closed enclosure's inside air. New
  • HDMI-53The +/-12 V converter's start-up surge lasts about 8 ms, not 1-2 ms, and adds about 0.1-0.25 A at the 5 V port pin while it ramps. New
  • HDMI-54The printed paste can fill only about 20-25 % of each Micro-HDMI shell slot, so the 32 shell joints rely on a top fillet. New
  • HDMI-55C5's lands are 0.4 mm shorter and 0.35 mm narrower than Panasonic's recommended pattern for this 8 x 10.2 mm can. New
  • HDMI-56107 back-side header labels are 0.8 mm tall, below JLC's 1.0 mm minimum text height, so they may print unreadable. New
  • HDMI-57DRC still cannot see the courtyards of 50 BRK8310-derived footprints, but a direct check finds no body overlaps among them. New
  • HDMI-58A shorted or ten-times-low R711-R718 silently removes that port's current limit, so a later cable short draws amps through pin 19 or makes all eight heads restart repeatedly. New

Frontend FPGA Board14 layers V7 · edited 13 Nov 2026, 11:15 CST
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • FE-43R13 still has no JLC stock and the evening fix plan sends it to outside sourcing, but in-stock 0.1 % parts can set both GTP rails without it. New
  • FE-02High-case stack load at the 5 V pin comes within about 0.02-0.10 A of the 0.63 A minimum port current limit.
  • FE-03The Frontend uses the commercial 0-85 C FPGA by the owner's choice, and no thermal estimate shows it stays below 85 C.
  • FE-05Boot flash U7 (W25Q128JWYIQ) has no JLC stock and is a special-order package at Winbond.
  • FE-06Two out-of-stock parts are feedback resistors (R10, R13); building without them drives GTP rails above absolute max.
0 blockers5 majors23 minors19 notes

New in V3, smaller items

  • FE-47Vivado lists the fitted W25Q128JW-Q for Artix 7 x1 boot (FE-16 closes), but no W25Q64 part, so the FE-05 fallback flash would not program with the standard flow. New
  • FE-48Four pull-ups are outside the values AMD and TI specify: PROGRAM_B, INIT_B and FCS_B use 4.99 kOhm where UG470 asks for 4.7 kOhm or less, and the TPS74801 power-good node uses 4.99 kOhm where TI requires 10 kOhm to 1 MOhm. New
  • FE-49JLC has not confirmed its bottom-side rule; under the 180-minus rule seven bottom parts would land turned 180 degrees, though the remark now gives a pin-1 table. New
  • FE-53The remark now asks JLC for the flatness the DF40 connectors need, but JLC has not agreed, and even 0.5 % bow allows about 0.036 mm across J1/J2. New
  • FE-54Several small parts and the XADC supply lack the nearby bypass capacitor their makers show: U8's 1.8 V side, Y1, U14, U15's 1.5 V side, and VCCADC (no 100 nF). New
  • FE-55The Frontend carries no board name, revision, date or reference designators, so assembled boards cannot be told apart by eye. New
  • FE-56Y2's datasheet covers only a 15 pF load, so its drive into the 830 Ohm level-shift divider is not specified. New
  • FE-57Three Frontend mirror-layer pairs differ by 72-75 points of copper, outside JLC's balance guidance, but the bow this causes across J1/J2 is under 1 um nominal and about 4-6 um at worst. New
  • FE-59Option B's 23.6 mm-long 120-position J5 needs bow under about 0.10 % to keep Hirose's 0.02 mm, while JLC's 0.75 % allows 0.15 mm; the FPGA-LDO boards' own L2/L5 copper imbalance adds only a few um. New

Closed

  • FE-44The placement file turns the FPGA U1 by 90 degrees against JLC's library footprint, and every ball still lands on a pad.
  • FE-13The schematic names the industrial-grade FPGA, but the JLC order places the commercial grade from inventory.
  • FE-51J3's placement point is 1.06 mm from the origin of JLC's footprint, so JLC would set the connector off its pads and slots.
  • FE-58The PCB remark does not say whether JLC may add balancing copper, though five signal layers hold only 6.5-10 % copper and two of them carry the 88-93 ohm cable pairs.
  • FE-04Three fitted 0805 capacitors under the Frontend leave 0.05 mm worst-case clearance to the FPGA-LDO board.
  • FE-08If a design drives FPGA ball M15 (EN_GTPWR) low, the FPGA resets itself and can reboot in a loop.
  • FE-14The pin constraints omit the configuration settings, GTP pin locations and TX polarity the board needs.
  • FE-27The J3 footprint was never checked against the 46765-1001 drawing; JLC links the drawing of another variant.
  • FE-45The bottom assembly drawing marks U7's /CS ball (B4) as 'pin 1 (A1)', so the only A1 guide for the boot flash points away from the real A1 corner.
  • FE-50Five bottom parts (U8, U11, U15, U17, U18) are 90 degrees off JLC's library footprints under every bottom-side reading.
  • FE-16It is not established that Vivado can program the W25Q128JW boot flash in x1 mode.

FPGA-LDO PCB · Option ALayout V16 · edited 13 Nov 2026, 09:49 CST
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • LDOA-39Option B's LDO board V2 copies Option A's ±10 V supply exactly, so the LDOA-08/LDOA-09 power-down polarity problem applies to Option B too, and neither Option B board has a clamp. New
  • LDOA-41Option A's CLK32 edges at the FPGA can cross the LVCMOS15 band twice when 10-14 ASIC outputs switch with them, and the J3/J4 joints keep part of that risk after the J1/J2 fix. New
  • LDOA-42Assuming 25 ohm, 1 ns ASIC drivers, 10-14 switching ASIC outputs can put about 0.6-1.1 V of ground bounce, mostly from the LDOA-05 J1/J2 trunk, on the idle-low STIM_START and STIM_EN inputs at the ASIC. New
  • LDOA-43On Option A the only pulls on the ASIC control lines are on FPGA-LDO A, so a J3 or J4 joint lifted about 0.5-0.65 mm at its pin-80 end leaves four fully powered ASICs with both resets, IMP_TST, AC_IN and SPI_LATCH floating, and the stimulator controls float by about 0.73 mm, once four rails have opened. New
  • LDOA-44The far ASICs get their stimulator and reset controls through a second unretained DF40 joint, out of reach of any pull on or below the near board, so lifting one end of that joint 0.50-0.76 mm can float them while rails stay connected. New
  • LDOA-02The L2 AGND plane is the return plane for many L1 digital lanes, so their edge currents flow in analog ground.
  • LDOA-059 of the 15 Frontend ground pins on J1/J2 still reach the ground planes only through thin top-layer traces.
  • LDOA-06LT3582 VIN pin 5 has no local input capacitor; it is fed through two microvias and 8.6 mm of 0.3 mm L5 trace.
  • LDOA-07U1 LT3582EUD-12#PBF is still out of stock at JLC; the order files now forbid other versions.
  • LDOA-08ASIC_VDD10 has no clamp diode to AGND, so a power-down can pull the LT3042 output below its -0.3 V limit.
  • LDOA-09On a light-load power-down, U10 (LT3094) output falls over 0.3 V below its input, past its absolute maximum.
  • LDOA-10LT3582 ripple crossing the single tie NT1 puts about 0.4-1.3 mV rms between the ASIC's AGND and DGND pins.

New in V3, smaller items

  • LDOA-38The FPGA-LDO A panel is now drawn, but three break-off tabs end within 0.6 mm of fitted capacitors and JLC has not confirmed the panel. New
  • LDOA-45The 3.3 V contact J2.69 sits next to STIM_START_SHARED on J2.67, so one solder bridge puts 3.3 V on a 1.5 V FPGA output and all eight ASIC inputs. New
  • LDOA-46Three polarized bottom parts (U1, D12, J4) are at 0 or 180 degrees in the CPL, and a half-turn on U1 or D12 shorts the 3.3 V input or drives the -12 V rail positive. New
  • LDOA-47The +12 V test pad TP2 is 0.6 mm from the enable pad TP4, so one probe slip puts about 12 V on enable pins rated 6-6.5 V. New
  • LDOA-51The LT3582 layout leaves out the ground-plane cuts and the extra exposed-pad vias of data sheet Figure 12, so its switching currents spread through the L3/L4 GND planes. New
  • LDOA-53The ASIC1 CLK32 lane and the STIM_CLK/EN/START trio run about 8 mm on L6 at a 0.10 mm gap, 0.30 mm above their plane, the worst lane crosstalk on Option A. New
  • LDOA-54The L2 analog ground plane is missing under all of ASIC8's clock lane, 40 % of ASIC7's and 37 % of ASIC5's. New
  • LDOA-55ASIC7's clock runs 2.7 mm over the J4 port's top-chip SPI latch, and a data lane runs 2.3 mm over that port's top-chip SPI clock. New
  • LDOA-56LDOB-07's fix moves the pulls to the router, which still leaves them across router J3/J4 and the near-to-far joint. New
  • LDOA-57A J3/J4 joint lifted at the pin-1 end, or rocked toward the probe edge, can open all four grounds while rails, including -10 V, stay connected. New
  • LDOA-60C1001 and C901 probably fall below the LT3094's 10 uF and the LT3042's 4.7 uF minimum input capacitance at 12 V bias; only the output side was being tracked. New
  • LDOA-61The LT3042/LT3094 symbols still have no data sheet revision. New

Closed

  • LDOA-01No orderable part is chosen for inductors L11/L12, so the +/-12 V converter cannot be assembled as ordered.
  • LDOA-13The +/-10 V stimulation rails can reach +/-10.3 V, with no ASIC or capacitor rating to show that is safe.
  • LDOA-15One RC starts every ASIC rail with no FPGA control or power-good; the +/-10 V rails need up to about 0.5 s.
  • LDOA-18All 3.3 V input current for the board passes through a single 0.15 mm through via.
  • LDOA-28Nothing discharges the ±10 V ASIC rails, so they can stay charged for seconds after the 1.5 V rails fall.
  • LDOA-49Pin map V6 also needs a pull-down on each port's new top-chip SPI_LATCH line (J3/J4 pin 70), which V12 routes as a plain data lane.
  • LDOA-62The Frontend pin constraints set no DRIVE or SLEW on the FPGA outputs to the ASICs, so their edge rate is never chosen.
  • LDOA-37On FPGA-LDO A V12, Frontend C4, C42 and C59 sit over masked ±12 V stimulation copper, so if a worst-case contact wears through the mask, C4 could push +12 V into the FPGA core (1.1 V absolute maximum), while C42 and C59 would mainly short the -12 V supply.
  • LDOA-23D11 and D12 (B0540WS-7) are out of stock at JLC, and the proposed B5819WS substitute is unchecked.
  • LDOA-27The order sets no bottom stencil thickness; above 0.10 mm the X2SON pin apertures release paste poorly.
  • LDOA-50U1 LT3582EUD-12#PBF is Active and in stock at DigiKey (359 pcs, 10-week lead time), so U1 can be bought now; only the '-12 only' order note is still missing.
  • LDOA-03Two stacked copies of the only ASIC board on file would short data outputs and leave ASIC3/4/7/8 unconnected.
  • LDOA-04Option A has four SPI data pairs for eight ASICs, so each near/far ASIC pair gets identical configuration.

ASIC boards · top and bottomV7 · edited 13 Nov 2026, 00:07 CST
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • AB-03All 119 AGND F.Cu-to-In1 laser vias on each board use 0.25 mm pads on 0.15 mm holes, some sit close to the edge or to other holes, and PCBWay, the fabricator now chosen, has not confirmed them.
  • AB-04On each board 40 via rings lie within 0.25 mm of the routed edge (closest 0.10 mm, hole 0.15 mm), and 19 rail vias touch their pulled-back plane only at its edge; 18 of these are the only path to the rail for one die pad each.
  • AB-05Hole-to-plane gaps (0.127 mm), 3 mil tracks, 0.05 mm via rings and hole pairs are below PCBWay's published normal limits for ten layers, and the KiCad rules equal the drawn values, so DRC cannot flag them.
  • AB-06In every stacked pair the upper board's 24 under-die capacitors leave only 1.00 mm (worst case) above the lower board for mask, die attach, die and probe base, not the 1.15 mm in A-1.
  • AB-09The ASIC boards carry no board name and the DF40 has no key, so two boards of one kind tie 22-24 chip outputs together and a near board turned 180° on its FPGA-LDO port puts the ±8 V stimulation rails on chip outputs.
  • AB-11The ASIC designer chose ENEPIG at PCBWay, but PCBWay has not confirmed ENEPIG together with laser vias on a 0.8 mm ten-layer board.
  • AB-12Nothing in the V4 package tells the bonding house where, which way or how to bond the dies: there is no die outline, centre, orientation mark or V4 bond diagram, and no wire, attach or encapsulation specification.
  • AB-13The 0.1016 mm bond fingers are below two published wire-bond guides and were bonded only on the one-die June board, and V4's 33 shared middle fingers (0.378 mm long) each need two opposed stitches with about 0.19 mm of finger per stitch, which nobody has shown.
  • AB-14The opened finger rows leave bare ±8 V stimulation copper 0.08-0.10 mm apart, so an encapsulant is required, but the rows end 0.41-0.50 mm from three routed edges, which leaves no room for a dam to hold it.
  • AB-15With the die placed as on the June board, each probe crosses a 0.47 mm strip of board with no keep-out, over all eight inner planes and, on the die face, an F.Cu CLKH track and capped VDD_DRIVER/REF_VDD vias (chip 1) or the AGND pour and five AGND blind vias (chip 2, whose CLKH is on B.Cu); V4's finger ring sits 0.31 mm farther from the probe edge than June's.
  • AB-18None of the 30 capacitors on either board has a capacitance value, voltage rating or part number, including four 0201 parts on the ±10 V rails.
  • AB-42Both ASIC boards are now ten layers in 0.8 mm, which PCBWay's public table puts outside its normal and medium range; PCBWay has not confirmed the stack.
0 blockers12 majors14 minors8 notes

New in V3, smaller items

  • AB-32No build order or cleaning rule is recorded, although Hirose advises against cleaning the DF40s and the bond fingers sit 2.35 mm from J4's solder joints. New
  • AB-37KiCad ERC cannot check the V4 pin map, because every die pin and every connector pin is typed passive. New

Closed

  • AB-01FPGA-LDO A V12, the Option B router V2 and the Frontend still follow pin map V5, so 6 top-chip outputs per port (12 per stack) reach no FPGA pin and ASIC3/4/7/8 cannot be fully read; their SPI, latch and clock inputs already reach FPGA balls and need only a new pin file.
  • AB-07Both boards still fit the generic J1 side pin at the same spot, so in each pair one tail lands on the other board's lead. That can hold the DF40 joint open by up to about 0.49 mm, using up its 0.45 mm contact travel at the worst tolerance corner.
  • AB-19On the Frontend (not an ASIC board), the bottom 0805 capacitors C4, C42 and C59, up to 1.45 mm tall, can press up to 0.10 mm into the FPGA-LDO board at the DF40's 1.35 mm minimum mated height.
  • AB-27Pin map V6 gives the top chips their own SPI_LATCH line without the 10 kΩ pull-down that holds the bottom chips' latch low before the FPGA is configured.
  • AB-0220 (top) / 22 (bottom) F.Cu-to-In2..In8 blind vias (0.35-1.41 mm deep, 0.15 mm hole) are deeper than any laser or controlled-depth process JLCPCB or PCBWay lists; 16/19 sit on the B.Cu control bus, 4/3 have no copper below them, and 0.8 mm alone does not fix them.
  • AB-08Both V4 files are a 1.6 mm ten-layer stack, while the fit models and records use 0.8 mm. The choice changes stack height, probe-row spacing and the via build.
  • AB-16The top board's 28 chip lines on In4 run inside the REF_VCM plane, between the REF_VDD and VDD10/VDD_DRIVER planes. They couple about 5.4-5.8 pF into REF_VDD, 9.4-10 pF into In5 and 2.4-2.8 pF into REF_VCM, and a DGND pour on In4 alone removes only about a third of the plane coupling.
  • AB-22No chip's DISC_OUT reaches the FPGA, and the top chips' DISC_OUT pads have no copper at all, while the ASIC designer has not said whether DISC_OUT must be read, grounded or left open.
  • AB-28Pin map V6 adds two FPGA outputs per port for the top chips' SPI clock and latch, although the ASIC designer's sheet marks SPI_CLK and SPI_LATCH as shared.
  • AB-30J3, J4 and the second chip lie outside the A2 sheet on both V4 schematics, so the schematic PDFs show neither connector nor U01_BCD2.
  • P-SPITop chips shared the bottom chips' SPI streams (V2/V3)
  • P-B2a28 crossing via-span pairs made the stack unbuildable
  • P-M1Two chips drove each DISC_OUT pin (3 and 5)
  • P-M5Open via holes in the DF40 pads
  • P-M6Solder paste on the bond fingers, zero-margin mask opening per finger, U01 placed by the assembler
  • pair-layout F12VSS10 F.Cu-In6 via typed 'buried'
  • R2L-07V3 In4 re-route: off-centre track ends, 3W pairs, split crossings
  • pair-power F9Rail pins 36, 54 and 56 (and 38 on the bottom board) had only one via
