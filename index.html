The opening view uses stacked_headboard_compact_B_v1(1).stl, supplied for this presentation on September 22, 2026. The original bytes are preserved losslessly; the viewer does not shorten, scale individual parts, or reconstruct the design.

The A/B/C/D region labels follow the repository’s assembly guide: A is the ASIC-carrier stack, B+C the routing PCB, and D the FPGA PCB. Colors and label positions are presentation annotations, not fields stored in the STL.

This is an assembly concept. The STL does not encode physical units, and no manufacturing dimensions or validated electrical interfaces are inferred from it.
