System overview

Model provenance and presentation scope

The supplied stacked_headboard_compact_B_v1(1).stl is preserved byte for byte. The browser uses all 650 source triangles. Camera animation changes the view, not the model.

The former B bridge region and C lower board are grouped as one routing/LDO subsystem per Howard’s September 22 instruction. The source model is not edited. The overview receiver stack retains earlier explanatory geometry; current FPGA, LDO and carrier views are shown separately in their subsystem tabs. The dated adapter view is historical; the adapter detail view uses derived Opal Kelly XEM8310 and BRK8310 models and labels the proposed interposer separately. Neither view proves mating fit. The R2 direct-carrier option and earlier KR260 concept remain in dated history.

Names are reported workstream assignments from the repository’s September 18 follow-up. They do not replace its separate, still-unassigned layout reservation and reviewer records.
