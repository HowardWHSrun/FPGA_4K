The designer's 80-pin order against our FPGA-LDO A V12 and Frontend 14 layers V3 · drawn 12 Nov 2026, 11:23 CST

Every pin passes straight through the bottom board to the top board. The bottom board's chips already match our boards. The top board's chips do not: our boards were drawn for an older order with 6 fewer wires per port.

To settle with the designer

  1. Is this 80-pin order final?
  2. May pins 70 / 72 (top SPI latch, clock) be tied to pins 61 / 63? That cuts 12 new wires to 8.
  3. Are pins 3 / 5 (DISC_OUT) needed? The top chips' DISC_OUT is not on the connector.
  4. What drives pin 7 (CLKH) and pin 1 (VDD_CGEN)?
  5. Which chip is "(2)", and is Data 1 or Data 8 the first bit?
  6. Idle state of the shared control pins.
