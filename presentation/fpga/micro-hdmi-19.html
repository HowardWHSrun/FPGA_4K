DESIGN STATUS / 01 Proposed for the 50T board

Eight data contacts are still unconnected in the latest 50T schematic. A matching MCU-side board is proposed to supply the protected 5 V input; its available current is unknown. The preserved 100T checkpoint is a separate design.

!
This is a custom electrical link, not an HDMI video port.

Contact 19 is proposed as a protected 5 V input, while the native 50T schematic still labels its net LINK_12V. The 28 September review questioned the inherited 12 V label but approved no final cable voltage. Resolve that mismatch before implementation. Conventional Type-D HDMI also assigns +5 V to contact 19, but an ordinary HDMI port is not a qualified power source for this custom map. Never connect ordinary HDMI equipment.

03 / BEFORE THIS IS A CABLE CONTRACT

What still needs to be checked

Electrical link

Assign the eight data contacts in the schematic, define the transmitter/receiver endpoints, coupling, protection, timing and receiver firmware.

Power path

Revise the native LINK_12V assignment before adopting 5 V. Measure the board-plus-ASIC load and qualify the source, cable, return path, voltage drop, faults and startup. Molex rates this receptacle at 0.8 A maximum per contact: one 5 V positive contact allows at most 4 W ideal connector-only, before cable or conversion losses. System current is unknown.

Physical continuity

Confirm the exact receptacle and cable part numbers, mating orientation, every contact, shell and PCB footprint against manufacturer drawings.
