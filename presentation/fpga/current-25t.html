03 / CUSTOM RECORDING FPGA · SELECTED DEVICE

XC7A25T on each of three remote boards.

Unrouted PCB

The selected device is XC7A25T-2CSG325I. Three separate custom FPGA PCBs are proposed, each with one custom 19-contact µHDMI cable to the XEM8310 interposer. The current native PCB retains the earlier 36 × 38 mm placement and two rigid DF40 connectors; its copper is unrouted and the DF40 interface is under redesign.

INTERFACE TO THE XEM8310

Same cable allocation on each board.

Recording and control

J4 contacts 3/5 and 6/8 send recording data on two active GTP transmitter pairs. 9/11 is a third transmitter pair physically wired but reserved in baseline firmware. 12/14 receives framed control from XEM. The custom FPGA generates ASIC CLK, DATA and LATCH locally.

Power and programming

J4.19 is a proposed 12 V input for the FPGA, routing and ASIC assembly. 1 is the target’s 1.8 V JTAG reference output; 2, 15, 17 and 18 are TMS, TDI, TCK and TDO. Source protection, current, return and a final programming method remain open.

The three cables enter separate XEM GTY banks: board A → 226, B → 225 and C → 224. Open the exact MC3 contact map ↗

BEFORE FABRICATION

Electrical and mechanical work remains.

Routing and power

The native 25T PCB has no tracks or vias. It needs controlled-impedance GTP routes, a reviewed stackup, power-entry protection, ASIC rail budget and layout/DRC review.

ASIC mating interface

The existing two rigid DF40 sites are placement references. A floating-connector redesign, return contacts and power feed still need an approved carrier-to-FPGA map.

Firmware and link

The 25T resource fit, GTY/GTP configuration, lane alignment and XEM receiver gateware require Vivado builds and hardware tests. The aggregate channel count across the three boards is still being confirmed.

Prior design history

The 28 September 50T board and purchasing review remains online as a historical placement checkpoint. Its BOM and selected device are not the current 25T choice.
