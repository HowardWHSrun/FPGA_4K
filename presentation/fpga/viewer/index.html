Move around: Ctrl + scroll to zoom; drag with the middle or right mouse button to pan. Use the + / − buttons on touch screens. Inspect: click a part, then use Parts, Nets or Layers. The initial view shows front copper and silkscreen; Layers includes front, back and all-layer presets. “Flip board” mirrors the view; “Fit board” returns to the whole outline.

This browser view reads unchanged native PCB files. KiCanvas is an alpha viewer and does not implement every KiCad 10 feature; the original KiCad project remains the authority for dimensions, connectivity and manufacturing checks. The viewer does not display missing connections as a fabrication verdict.
