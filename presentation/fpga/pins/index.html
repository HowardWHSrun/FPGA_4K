How to read this map

Positions come from native KiCad pad centers, viewed from the PCB front. Back-side components are shown as seen through the board; this is not a connector mating-face drawing. Repeated G or SH labels are multiple physical pads on one electrical pin.

Assigned status is reported per net: a gray assigned pin may already be connected locally. The current routing audit supplies copper-completion evidence. The 102 endpoints without a functional net comprise 83 intentional NCs and 19 future-interface reservations. U1.L9/L10 and U2–U5.4 now belong to GND in CAD; A13 has an NC marker after removal of its direct AC_IN path. J5.46 is an analog reservation, not a completed source circuit. The board is not fabrication-ready.
