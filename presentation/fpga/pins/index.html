How to read this map

Positions come from native KiCad pad centers, viewed from the PCB front. Back-side components are shown as seen through the board; this is not a connector mating-face drawing. Repeated G or SH labels are multiple physical pads on one electrical pin.

Assigned status is reported per net: a gray assigned pin may already be connected locally. There are still 146 missing assigned-net connections. Unassigned pins are not automatically spare. The six saved CAD NC pins need correction or review: U1.L9/L10 require ground, while U2–U5.4 need grounding review. The board is not fabrication-ready.
