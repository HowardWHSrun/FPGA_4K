Applied changes

What changed in R39

Land patterns and silkscreen

Retains 323 supplier-supported land corrections and improved silkscreen. The remaining fine-pitch TI/Hirose lands keep their accurate supplier geometry; JLCPCB’s exact-part library corroborates these narrow-land families.

FPGA routing rules

Fine escape rules are confined to the FPGA area. Ordinary spacing and unrelated bottom-side pads retain their existing protections. The adjacent laser-via candidate uses 0.35 mm lands and 0.125 mm drills.

Component information

Corrects 31 engineering and purchasing fields on 11 components, including actual regulator nominal voltages and the manufacturer’s current alias for six capacitors.

Native checks

Physical DRC, ERC and schematic/PCB parity pass under the delivered project settings. All existing placement, pad assignments and 113 surface track segments are preserved.

“Unconnected” means awaiting PCB copper. The schematic net assignments are intact. Native connectivity counts 1,413 PCB connections awaiting copper; the DRC listing is capped at 499. There are no placed vias or completed copper planes.

The eight-layer 3+2+3 HDI construction remains an engineering candidate. JLCPCB must issue the stack, via and impedance/stencil process before critical routing and fabrication. One consolidated request is prepared and unsent. Passing CAD checks does not establish fabrication or powered-operation readiness.
