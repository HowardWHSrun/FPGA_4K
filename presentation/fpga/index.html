ASIC clock candidate: Y2 could also feed a fabric MMCM to generate 32 MHz for eight ASIC outputs after FPGA configuration. This is a calculated topology, not implemented HDL or a timing guarantee; check ASIC startup needs. Read the clock study ↗

Layered escape plan: ASIC contacts are assigned to proposed L1/L3/L6 corridors for later breakout; L2/L5/L7 are reserved for ground returns and L4 for power in the proposed stack. These are planning assignments, not traces. Open final contact-by-layer map ↗

04 / Before ordering

Resolve the design choices

The 50T board is useful for review now. These items determine whether its candidates can become an orderable BOM.

  1. Complete the selected 12 V power contract. Establish the FPGA plus ASIC load and routing-board feed, then design the protected source, cable and rail calculations together.
  2. Close connector identities. Approve both DF40T mating pairs, their spacing and alignment, plus J4 and the custom cable drawing.
  3. Approve FPGA and passive order codes. Select the complete XC7A50T suffix and exact codes for every TBD line.
  4. Complete the GTP/GTY design. The 3-TX/1-RX contact mapping is selected; verify reference clock, XEM GTY implementation, cable polarity, coupling and ESD, then route and check the PCB.
  5. Qualify manufacturing and power. Close ERC/DRC, current and thermal budgets, startup and shutdown, measurements at the FPGA pins, cable tests and supplier stackup.
