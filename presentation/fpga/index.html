ONE CABLE / THREE PATHS

Power first. Then configure and record.

1. Negotiate power

The controller boots before the FPGA. It checks the source, requests power and enables the protected main supply.

2. Configure the FPGA

USB2 commands reach 1.8 V JTAG through a level translator. Five bare fixture contacts recover the controller itself.

3. Record and send data

ASIC data enters the FPGA. Three proposed differential lanes and a clock reach the matching XEM receiver adapter.

Which pins are deliberately unused?

NC means intentionally not connected. These pins are separate from the unfinished copper count. SBU1/SBU2 and three spare mezzanine contacts are NC in this revision; grounded control pins are connected, not unused.
