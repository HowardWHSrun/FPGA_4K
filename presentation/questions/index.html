From question to decision

Add your name, then join the discussion.

  1. Identify yourself

    Enter your name so teammates can see who raised a question and who responded.

  2. Raise one clear question

    Give it a title, describe the uncertainty and add the relevant board, pin, source or proposed option. Name the person who needs to review it when known.

  3. Answer in the same thread

    Explain the reasoning and include supporting references. Follow-up questions stay with the original discussion.

  4. Record the outcome

    Update the status as the question progresses. When it is resolved, record the decision and who confirmed it; the thread remains available for later reviews.
