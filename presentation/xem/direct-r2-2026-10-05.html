Connection scope

Review the complete connector arrangement

MC1, MC2 and MC3

MC1 has 100 contacts; MC2 and MC3 each have 80. Every contact has an audited native pad and schematic disposition. MC3 connects its 28 DGND contacts; the other 52 contacts are explicitly reserved / no-connect. They do not implement a GTH link.

Original LDO board and power

Both J1 and J19 are included for direct mating. J1 carries the mapped ASIC interface and named rails. J19 is preserved as a mechanical mating pair with explicit no-connect contacts. The candidate supplies the module bank rails and keeps the ADC ground reference separate; source, current, sequencing and load qualification remain open.

What still needs qualification

Timing, power and assembled fit

Clock and data routing

The returned clock is 68.476 mm and D_1–D_8 span 69.757–79.765 mm of native copper: an 11.29 mm group spread, with different via counts. These lengths do not establish propagation delay or the provisional ≤100 ps spread. Stack-up, local returns, ASIC setup/hold/data-valid limits and Vivado timing remain open.

Independent power branches

J3 supplies XEM VDCIN; J4 supplies raw VIO 2.5 V ±2%; J5 supplies the original low-stage nominal 3.3 V feed. PG sequences the 1.5 V bank regulator and does not monitor ASIC rails. Actual load, limiting/startup, drop, plating and thermal behavior need qualification.

The carrier includes no automatic powered-off interface isolation, ASIC rail-ready monitor, ASIC hardware current limiter or accepted backfeed/stimulation cutoff. The original high-stage supplies and AGND return still need their accepted source/sequence contract. Firmware high-Z alone does not qualify the power sequence. Exact simultaneous seating, factory DFM and powered recording remain open.
