How the boards attach

Carrier · current R15 CADXEM8305 · simplified reference bodyGerald LDO · source outline and component envelopes

The XEM8305 sits on the carrier’s MC1, MC2 and MC3 connectors. The four mounting positions register the module to the carrier. J1 and J19 face the LDO assembly on the opposite side; the barrel input and carrier USB-C JTAG remain accessible at the edges.

Use the separation slider to reveal both connector interfaces. At 0 mm, the XEM uses a nominal 5 mm mating gap above the carrier and the LDO a nominal 2 mm gap below it. Gerald’s matching native LDO outline, nine regulator bodies and 52 passive component envelopes are included; parts marked DNP in its source BOM are omitted. The LDO’s narrow tail extends 18.425 mm beyond the carrier edge, as in the source geometry. These simplified bodies explain the arrangement; component heights, terminal seating, standoffs and populated clearance still require mechanical verification.

Power and routing

12 V input

The barrel input supplies XEM VDCIN through MC1 and the carrier’s 3.3 V converter. The main inner 12 V feed grows from 2.5 to 4.2 mm in R15.

3.3 V for the LDO assembly

The converter supplies the LDO/ASIC assembly. The main inner 3.3 V corridor grows from 3.0 to 4.8 mm. Narrow pad escapes and sensitive converter returns retain their local limits.

FPGA bank supply

The XEM module’s 1.8 V output feeds the selected I/O-bank supply connections. This is separate from the XEM’s 12 V main input and the carrier’s 3.3 V LDO feed.

USB-C JTAG

The bridge circuit follows the native BRK8305 reference with an edge-facing USB socket. EEPROM provisioning and programming in Vivado remain bring-up tasks.
