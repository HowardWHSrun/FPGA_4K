PROVENANCE & SCOPE

What this view does—and does not—show.

The preserved mesh is hardware/assembly/stacked_headboard_original.stl, indexed into model-data.js. The original has 650 triangles and an envelope of 65 × 24 × 15.75 model units. STL does not specify units.

The compact view is a new parametric presentation study: it shortens the free bridge interval [3, 18] in x, keeps the ASIC side unchanged, and translates the downstream block. The default retained span is 50%, chosen only to illustrate the request. It is not a recovered or approved shortened CAD revision. Connector footprints are not redesigned.

A–D labels follow the repository’s assembly guide; colors and highlighting are presentational. They do not establish component part numbers, pin mapping, mechanical fit, PCB dimensions or manufacturing readiness. The existing FPGA working draft is separately documented as incomplete.
