Open [the presentation](index.html) from a static HTTPS host. The eight slides include native interactive PCB layouts, source-backed 3D models and complete one-page schematics. [Presenter notes](notes.md) are included.

The FPGA V41 board is 25 × 23 mm; separate LDO V5 is 32 × 32.5 mm; combined V6 is 35 × 35 mm. The separate option uses two mating mezzanine connector pairs; the combined PCB retains the circuits and replaces those connectors with internal signal connections. Power inputs remain independent.

CAD illustrations and saved routing are design-review material. They do not establish manufacturing readiness, electrical operation or qualified populated fit. Native model and source checksums are retained in the viewer metadata. Historical native revision identifiers are preserved.

Third-party viewer/font/model licenses are retained alongside their assets. This bundle includes only runtime assets; engineering source projects, libraries, source snapshots and working files remain outside it. Native PCB files are included because the interactive KiCanvas layout reads them directly.
