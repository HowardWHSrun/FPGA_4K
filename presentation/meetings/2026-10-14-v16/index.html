Carrier board schematic

  • Power12 V powers XEM8305; an onboard buck provides 3.3 V to the LDO board.
  • PinoutEight data lines plus clocks, SPI, reset and stimulation control at 1.8 V.
  • ProgrammingUSB-C JTAG bridge with 1.8 V level translation. USB powers the bridge only.
  • ConnectorsMC1/MC2 connect XEM8305; the 50-pin J1 links the LDO board.
