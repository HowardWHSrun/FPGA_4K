- Breakout: active `BRK8310_Custom_V1/project/BRK8310_Custom_V1.kicad_pcb` snapshot SHA-256 `6e806259935ebb2516774259a571e54d5e66fa0cad0a035a622bd1f0f39bcef0`; exported through KiCad without editing. It is an unrouted working design. The eight cable starts use J101–J108 footprint origins and leave toward the physical left-facing mouths; curves are visual approximations, not cable fit evidence.
- XEM8310: 29 September Opal Kelly unsimplified source-derived geometry in `2026-09-29_FPGA_Work/14_Opal_Kelly_3D_Mechanical_References/derivatives/`. Geometry is from the official model; board/component colors are illustrative. Its nominal registration uses native XYZ mapping `x=232.326−xXEM`, `y=7.905+zXEM`, `z=135.154−yXEM` in KiCad millimeters. This yields 5.0 mm nominal PCB-surface separation based on the source mate, not a physical clearance check. Four XEM mounting centers match current PCB footprints within approximately 0.00042 mm. Verify the actual board, mating connectors, clearances and stack heights.
- Frontend PCB: FPGA V60 public GLB from `2026-10-20_FPGA_Work/17_Wednesday_Meeting_Slides_V2/site/assembly-v23/`. It predates the current FPGA interface schematic and its PCB was not updated to those changes. The LDO is detailed V13. Neither is a completed fabrication or hardware qualification.
- ASIC visual V5: PCB-5 outline, 352 bondout-pad positions and ASIC regions from the 20 October assembly `data/asic-layout.json`. The earlier profile uses 1.6 mm board substrate and 3.0 mm gap between adjacent ASIC boards. The thinner display concept uses 0.8 mm PCB and 1.5 mm ASIC-to-ASIC gap. Two nominal 8.1 × 7 mm die bodies use the original ASIC reference dimensions, with illustrative centering and 0.12 mm display height. See ASIC_MODEL_V5.md. These are illustrative and have not been selected or qualified; connector blocks are schematic, not manufacturer models. Upper LDO-to-ASIC spacing remains approximately 3.4 mm in the thin view to avoid falsely claiming that the unchanged FPGA/LDO geometry fits the same smaller gap. Even this is not a complete 3D collision check.
- SpikeGadgets' [MSH headstage manual, p.4](https://spikegadgets.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/MSH_headstage_manual_Rev2a_0223.pdf) identifies 70/80-pin stacking connectors, but does not specify board thickness or mating gap. [Hirose DF40 catalog](https://www.hirose.com/product/document?documentid=en_DF40_CAT&documenttype=Catalog&lang=en&series=DF40) provides 1.5 mm mated-height family examples; it does not establish the SpikeGadgets part or validate this assembly.

All eight frontend stacks share same source geometry for conceptual system context. Relative cable paths, board placement and separations are illustrative; this viewer does not prove mechanical fit, signal integrity, thermal behavior or powered operation. No KiCad project was changed.

V5 uses fresh BRK export including silkscreen, the unsimplified official-derived XEM8310 GLB, and an explicit one-to-one link inspector. Host asset hashes and export settings are in HOST_REFRESH_V5.json. The separation control moves the frontend cable endpoints with their FPGA connectors.

## V7 PC and PCIe context

Codex authored the schematic PC tower and internal expansion-slot geometry for this presentation. The PC is not to scale with the native boards. The logical PCIe path begins at J6's actual finger-array center, native `(135.3261,0.75,162.0536)` mm, and exits +native Y / +viewer Z before reaching the generic PC slot. The custom circuit is electrical PCIe x4 and retains the source x8-length card edge. No physical cable/riser/enclosure is selected. Sources: current custom J6 geometry and the [Opal Kelly BRK8310 PCIe reference](https://docs.opalkelly.com/xem8310/brk8310-breakout-board/pci-express-reference-design/), whose reference endpoint example must be adapted for the custom x4 design.

## Public V8 bundle

U100 in the custom breakout display uses an independently authored nominal rectangular envelope in the same local pose and source bounds. Its original supplier tessellation and unreachable geometry are omitted from this bundle. All 72,459 retained non-U100 accessor payloads are byte-identical to the source; [conversion evidence](Public_Model_Changes_V8.json) records hashes and bounds.
