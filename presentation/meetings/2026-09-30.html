Initial PC path: XEM USB / FrontPanel. BRK J6 PCIe is a future alternate mode because it shares banks 224/225.

Programming bridge to design

XEM GPIO is 1.2 V; target JTAG is 1.8 V. The proposed bridge needs translation, safe enable and probe-source selection.

First-port candidate MC3 contacts: 57 TMS · 59 TDI · 61 TCK · 63 TDO · 65 enable. A separate allocation for all three targets is still TBD.

TX/RX table headings use custom-FPGA directions. At XEM these are GTY RX0/RX1/RX2 and TX0. The TX and RX halves of XEM channel 0 are independent.

Working architecture

Three custom links to XEM

Two active recording pairs per board, a reserved third pair and reverse serial commands.

  1. Approve the ASIC / connector contract.Resolve IMP_TST, AC_IN, sharing, programming fanout, returns and the supply feed. Confirm ASIC / channel distribution across the three boards.
  2. Choose the three-target programming plan.XEM-generated JTAG with a safe bridge and explicit target selection; keep a usable recovery probe.
  3. Specify power and the actual cable.Protected 12 V source branches, entry/inrush control, cable continuity, load and return budget.
  4. Choose the layout and firmware proof path.Fabricator-qualified HDI or a revised breakout; Vivado fit/timing and total USB capture rate.

Meeting outcomes are pending. R10 is a feasibility option; its HDI process and routing are not an approved manufacturing baseline.
