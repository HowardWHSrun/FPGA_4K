Assembly references

FPGA V60 and LDO V13 retain the source registration and two direct 60-contact interface. U1/J1/J2/J3 poses equal V54; V60’s three local resistor placement changes are retained. The middle LDO lies between ASIC 3 and 2, with common probe-facing edges exposed. The host has separate interposer→BRK and XEM→interposer seating stages.

Gap readouts are nominal design references. V20 preserves all six V12 stack-connector poses and the +5 mm host registration. The cable path ends at the new J1 footprint, because its opening has no assigned source model. FPGA V60 has 1.8 V Bank 0/JTAG/flash and 1.5 V ASIC banks. The display does not qualify populated mating, routing, thermal behavior, electrical operation or animal fit.
