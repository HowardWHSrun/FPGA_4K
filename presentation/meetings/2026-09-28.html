03 / Interfaces

Checks needed before assigning pins

  • Power: verify the actual cable input voltage and source, all FPGA and GTP rails, and the clean feed to ASIC LDOs. The inherited 12 V label was questioned during the meeting.
  • Clock and boot: confirm the GTP differential reference clock, frequency, jitter and link-rate relationship from device documentation; choose flash capacity from the maximum configuration image size.
  • MCU link: count micro-HDMI contacts, grounds, power, high-speed pairs and low-speed lines from the actual connector. Allocate FPGA-to-MCU data and MCU-to-FPGA control, then decide whether JTAG uses cable contacts or separate pads.
  • Mechanical and test access: review two-connector placement, 60 versus 120 contacts, board orientation, mounting support and accessible FPGA I/O test points.

A three-transmit/one-receive high-speed split and use of low-speed lines for control were discussion options. They are not an approved micro-HDMI pin map.
