Zitong’s complete component list

Sources: Zitong_1007_Original.pptx, complete slide 1. Files and provenance

Zitong’s entire original slide is preserved without rewriting or cropping: FPGA and package, LEDs, reference clock, flash, inventory statements, comparison parts and the source boot-validation caveat. Inventory figures are dated source statements. Pan and zoom to discuss individual entries. Zitong's source calls XC7A35T-2CSG325I the final FPGA decision. Its requirements are XC7A series, at least two GTP lanes, more than 120 user I/Os, more than 100 units in stock, authorized AMD distributors and the smallest suitable package. It reports 122 units at JLCPCB and says packages smaller than 15 × 15 mm do not provide enough I/Os for this design. These are Zitong's dated selection claims, rather than current purchasing verification. M1 retains this FPGA and both indicators. The eight-chip statement describes the intended system, rather than demonstrated operation. This slide preserves Zitong's proposed clock list and order. The source says Jiaao confirmed that one oscillator can serve the FPGA and GTP clock domains. The oscillator is described as 3.2 × 2.5 mm, ±25 ppm and 186 fs RMS phase jitter typical. The splitter is described as 1:2 LVDS, 1.8 V, up to 2 GHz and VQFN-16 at 3 × 3 mm. Review comments belong here: the source asks for better than ±20 ppm while listing ±25 ppm, and RMS phase jitter does not establish its <10 ps peak-to-peak cycle-to-cycle jitter request. The reported oscillator characterization uses 3.3 V rather than M1's selected 1.8 V. The typed LMK1D2102LRGTR suffix differs from the source's linked LMK1D2102RGTR. M1 adopts the oscillator and AC coupling, but provisionally omits the LMK while the GTP ODIV2 fabric-clock route awaits Vivado verification. Listing the LMK here records Zitong's proposal, rather than claiming it is fitted in M1. This slide preserves Zitong's flash section and programming workflow. Its requirements are small size, a single SPI interface, Vivado recognition and Artix-7 support. The source gives the ISSI flash a 1.65–1.95 V supply and up to 166 MHz operation. It gives the NXP translator 1.08–1.95 V A-side, 1.65–3.6 V B-side, up to 208 MHz and XQFN-16 at 2.6 × 1.8 mm. Reported inventory and future distributor arrival dates are dated source claims and stay out of the slide body. The source explicitly says NVT4858 was designed for SD/SDIO and that FPGA boot/configuration behavior still needs validation. M1 adopts these exact parts with SPI x1, adapted idle biases and separate boot holds. Physical boot and brownout behavior remain open. The displayed programming path is the source's intended workflow, rather than a completed software demonstration.

The fabrication stackup and mechanical seating need supplier or physical evidence. The one-chip XEM path is the first integration step, with eight-chip scaling following verified results.
