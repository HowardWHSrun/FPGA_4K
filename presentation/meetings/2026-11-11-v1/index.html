Board comments

Open items per board after the 11 November review and the re-check with the new ASIC boards. Not fabrication approval.

Whole systemAll boards together
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • SYS-01Each cable's one GT lane must carry eight ASICs, but the only recorded line rate (1.25 Gb/s) counts one ASIC.
  • SYS-02High-load stacks reach the 637 mA port current limit on a 1.8 m cable with 30-32 AWG or thinner +5 V wire.
  • SYS-03No record estimates or limits head-stack heat; about 1.45 W per stack gives a 19-34 K still-air surface rise.
  • SYS-06Option B J5 carries 108 signals with only three return contacts: GND on pins 3 and 65 and AGND on pin 62.
  • SYS-07No head board has a fixing feature, so the stack and the HDMI cable load hang on unlocked DF40 connectors.
  • SYS-08Three 0805 capacitors under the Frontend can be 1.45 mm tall in the 1.5 mm gap, leaving 0.05 mm worst case.
  • SYS-09Unkeyed DF40 pairs let an ASIC board mate rotated 180 degrees, putting +/-10 V onto ASIC clock and data pins.
  • SYS-10The FPGA cannot switch or monitor the ASIC rails or +/-12 V converter; they are on whenever the cable is live.

Closed since the review

  • SYS-04Each stack has four stimulation SPI data pairs for eight ASICs, so paired near and far chips get one stream.
  • SYS-05No CAD exists for the far ASIC board that must drive the T_ lanes, so half the J3/J4 pin map is unchecked.

HDMI Extension BoardV37 · edited 10 Nov 2026, 13:38 CST
Closest to ready

Minor items

  • HDMI-01The port current limit is only 80 mA above the high-case Frontend load; a cable loop near 1 ohm uses it up.
  • HDMI-02The J4 barrel jack feeds the 7.5-15 V XEM8310 with no fuse, reverse-polarity or over-voltage protection.
  • HDMI-03U700 has thinner heat-spreading copper than TI recommends, and no thermal check exists for its high-case load.
  • HDMI-04The J920 test-hole row puts unfused 12 V beside ground and mismatched XEM8310 rails 0.71 mm apart.
  • HDMI-05U602 drives the FT232H TDO pin while the FT232H, in its default UART mode, also drives that pin.
  • HDMI-06Through a 2 m cable, Frontend JTAG works only up to about 5.5 MHz TCK, well below the common 15 MHz default.
  • HDMI-07U602 has no bypass capacitor closer than about 9.5 mm to either supply pin.
  • HDMI-08The U801-U816 ESD pads do not follow TI's DQA0010A pattern: signal pads are wider and ground pads narrower.
  • HDMI-09L3 is about 9.5 % copper against about 93 % on L2 and L4, and no balancing or bow/twist limit is decided.
  • HDMI-10Only two of the four XEM8310 standoff holes exist, so two module corners are held only by the connectors.
  • HDMI-11The eight Micro-HDMI mouths sit flush with the left edge, but the JLC note asks no +/-0.1 mm routing there.
  • HDMI-12MC3 has no JLC stock, and stock of MC1/MC2, C5 and the 0402 100 nF capacitor covers only 39 to 76 boards.
  • HDMI-1335 parts have no LCSC number in the schematic, and the C6 BOM line names a part other than its LCSC number.
  • HDMI-14JLC's +/-10 % thickness tolerance allows a 1.76 mm board, above the note's 1.70 mm limit at the fingers.
  • HDMI-15The PCIe fingers will be thin, soft ENIG immersion gold, not the hard gold card-edge contacts normally use.
  • HDMI-16210 vias have 0.15 mm drills (10.6:1), and 64 sit exactly at JLC's 0.20 mm inner hole-to-copper minimum.
  • HDMI-17Pin 19 may carry 0.55-0.77 A, but Molex rates it 0.3 A with all pins at 55 C and 0.8 A only at 25 C.
  • HDMI-18The Micro-HDMI mouths sit flush with the left edge, not 0.70 mm proud, and no left-edge tolerance is ordered.
  • HDMI-19U700 reaches its 125 C junction limit at about 5.3-5.65 A in 50 C air, inside the 5.0-5.7 A high-load cases.

Frontend FPGA Board14 layers V3 · edited 11 Nov 2026, 08:57 CST
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • FE-01Output capacitance on MPM3833C rails exceeds the datasheet's 100 uF maximum, clearly so on VCCINT (186 uF).
  • FE-02High-case stack load at the 5 V pin comes within about 0.02-0.10 A of the 0.63 A minimum port current limit.
  • FE-03The JLC BOM fits the commercial 0-85 C FPGA, and no thermal estimate shows the Frontend stays below 85 C.
  • FE-04Three fitted 0805 capacitors under the Frontend leave 0.05 mm worst-case clearance to the FPGA-LDO board.
  • FE-05Boot flash U7 (W25Q128JWYIQ) has no JLC stock and is a special-order package at Winbond.
  • FE-06Three out-of-stock parts are feedback resistors; building without them drives GTP rails above absolute max.
  • FE-07No panel is drawn, and the order remark sends break-off tabs to the east edge, 0.665 mm from J1's pads.

FPGA-LDO PCB · Option ALayout V12 · edited 10 Nov 2026, 16:06 CST
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • LDOA-01No orderable part is chosen for inductors L11/L12, so the +/-12 V converter cannot be assembled as ordered.
  • LDOA-02The L2 AGND plane is the return plane for many L1 digital lanes, so their edge currents flow in analog ground.
  • LDOA-05The 14 Frontend GND pins on J1/J2 reach the ground planes only through thin L1 traces and three laser vias.
  • LDOA-06LT3582 VIN pin 5 has no local input capacitor; it is fed through two microvias and 8.6 mm of 0.3 mm L5 trace.
  • LDOA-07U1 LT3582EUD-12#PBF is out of stock at JLC, and the order files do not forbid a non -12 substitute.
  • LDOA-08ASIC_VDD10 has no clamp diode to AGND, so a power-down can pull the LT3042 output below its -0.3 V limit.
  • LDOA-09On a light-load power-down, U10 (LT3094) output falls over 0.3 V below its input, past its absolute maximum.
  • LDOA-10LT3582 ripple crossing the single tie NT1 puts about 0.4-1.3 mV rms between the ASIC's AGND and DGND pins.
  • LDOA-11U3, U4 and U5 regulate against a different ground point from the ASIC's, so NT1 ripple lands on three rails.
0 blockers9 majors17 minors8 notes

Closed since the review

  • LDOA-03Two stacked copies of the only ASIC board on file would short data outputs and leave ASIC3/4/7/8 unconnected.
  • LDOA-04Option A has four SPI data pairs for eight ASICs, so each near/far ASIC pair gets identical configuration.

FPGA-LDO PCB · Option BRouter V2 + LDO board V2 · edited 10 Nov 2026, 16:08 CST
Not ready to order

Blockers and major items

  • LDOB-01J5 has only 2 GND and 1 AGND contacts for 108 single-ended ASIC lines, so return paths are long and shared.
  • LDOB-02All analogue ground current of the four ASIC boards returns to the regulators through one J5 contact, J5.62.
  • LDOB-0389 of 108 Option B lanes run over AGND on one board and GND on the other, pushing digital return into AGND.
  • LDOB-04Nothing tells the FPGA the ASIC rails are up, so a live mate can drive unpowered ASIC pins, in either option.
  • LDOB-05Neither Option B board file names a JLC stack-up, so the 0.10 mm laser vias are not tied to a valid template.
  • LDOB-06Option B's plug-in module is held to the head only by the J5 DF40 pair, which is rated for 30 mating cycles.
  • LDOB-07The stimulator pull-downs sit on the removable LDO board, so a module lifted at one end of J5 floats them.
  • LDOB-08Nothing on the head protects ASIC pins at router J5 from static discharge, and no mating procedure exists.
  • LDOB-09J5 has three return contacts for 80 ASIC outputs, so data bursts may put a double-crossing step on CLK32.
  • LDOB-10Five router rail pours run under 18 other-net J5 contacts, inside the strip Hirose reserves for each net.
  • LDOB-11Router J4 and Option A J4 run other-net tracks under the DF40C-80DS contacts, inside Hirose's own-net strip.

ASIC boards · top and bottomV3 · edited 11 Nov 2026, 20:35 CST
Inputs needed

Blockers and major items

  • A-1Capacitors under the dies: needs die thickness, bond-loop and encapsulation height, and the bonding house's OK for back-side parts under the bond area.
  • A-2Board thickness 1.6 or 0.8 mm still to decide with the fabricator's ten-layer stack; the files are 1.6 mm.
  • A-3The 30 capacitors have no value, rating or part number; the four on ±10 V need at least 16-25 V.
  • A-4Fabricator's written yes needed for blind vias L1 to L2..L9, filled and capped 0.15 mm via-in-pad, 3 mil track/gap, 0.05 mm ring, 0.127 mm hole to plane.
  • A-5Bonding house to confirm the finish (ENEPIG provisional) and the die-attach area; die size needed.
  • A-6Top board's chip lines run on In4 inside the REF_VCM plane; measure REF_VCM noise on the first board.

Closed since the review

  • P-B1Pin order now matches the FPGA-LDO boards (44 / 44 outputs per port).
  • P-B2Via set can be laminated: crossing via spans 28 to 0.
  • P-M1DISC_OUT no longer driven by two chips.
  • P-M2No extra FPGA outputs needed: top chips share SPI by design.
  • P-M5Via-in-pad holes set filled and capped.
