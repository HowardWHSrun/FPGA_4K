ASIC boards · top and bottom

V4 · edited 11 Nov 2026, 22:36 CST · the ASIC designer's pin order and separate SPI kept

Do not order yet: 2 blockers, 17 majors, 16 minors, 6 notes open. Most remaining majors wait on answers from the ASIC designer, the bonding house or the fabricator.

The ASIC boards V4 cannot be ordered yet. FPGA-LDO A, the Option B router and the Frontend still follow pin map V5, so 6 top-chip outputs per port reach no FPGA pin and the top chips need a new pin file (AB-01). In addition, 20 (top) / 22 (bottom) blind vias from F.Cu to In2-In8 cannot be built at 1.6 mm or at 0.8 mm (AB-02). Seventeen major items follow: the laser vias, edge vias and 3-mil features need a fabricator's written yes; the stack is tighter than recorded once the DF40's ±0.15 mm tolerance and the paired J1 pins are counted; nothing marks or keys the two board types; and nobody has written down how the dies are to be attached, finished, bonded, encapsulated and probed.
