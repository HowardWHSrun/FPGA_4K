FE-02Major

High-case stack load at the 5 V pin comes within about 0.02-0.10 A of the 0.63 A minimum port current limit.

All Frontend and FPGA-LDO power enters on one HDMI contact, J3.19, and passes through U16. Each HDMI port on the HDMI Extension Board has a TPS2553 switch that holds current constant at its limit; U16 is a buck converter, so if its input sags it draws more current, which can brown out or cycle the stack. Built up from the project's per-rail estimate, the load is comfortably below the limit in the expected case but close to it in the high case with all stimulators on, and the LDO board's +/-12 V start-up surge will briefly hit the limit at every power-up. No Vivado power figure or measurement exists, so the files cannot settle this; a measurement on the first Frontend + FPGA-LDO stack can.

FE-01Minor

With C4 now not fitted, VCCINT is under the MPM3833C's 100 uF maximum, but MPS has not confirmed the remaining output capacitance and feed-forward capacitor.

The MPM3833C datasheet lists 10 uF minimum, 22 uF typical and 100 uF maximum output capacitance, measured at one test point (1.2 V out, 2 A) on engineering samples. The module uses constant-on-time control (each switching pulse lasts a fixed time), and too much output capacitance can make its load-step response unstable. U2 on VCCINT has 186 uF fitted, still about 140-150 uF after the capacitors lose value under DC voltage, and also has a 22 pF feed-forward capacitor (C3, output to FB) that the MPS circuit does not show. VCCAUX (144 uF nominal) is borderline after derating, while VCCO and VIN_3V3 are probably below 100 uF effective; MPS does not show that any of these combinations is stable.

FE-25Minor

Four MPM3833C modules use 0603 input capacitors and have no added SW copper, against the maker's layout guide.

The MPM3833C buck module datasheet asks for an 0805 ceramic input capacitor as close to the pins as possible, wired directly to IN and PGND, and for extra copper on the SW (switch-node) pin to carry heat away. On U2, U3, U4 and U13 the input capacitors are 22 uF 0603 parts about 1.8 mm from the IN pin, and the SW pads have no copper beyond the pad itself. A smaller ceramic capacitor loses more of its capacitance under DC voltage (DC bias), so the 0603 parts give less effective capacitance at 3.3 V. The direct capacitor-to-IN/PGND connection is in place, and U16 already uses 0805 capacitors.

FE-34Note

MGTAVTT power-good is not used, so configuration can start without MGTAVTT being confirmed good.

MGTAVTT is the 1.2 V supply for the FPGA transceiver (GTP) terminations. The GTP termination calibration runs only once, during configuration, and needs every transceiver supply valid beforehand. U6 (MGTAVTT) has its power-good pin unconnected and is enabled by the same node that releases the reset supervisor U12, so configuration waits for VCCO_1V5 and MGTAVCC but not for MGTAVTT. It works by timing margin: the supervisor delay is at least ten times U6's soft-start ramp, so only a slow or failed U6 would be missed.
