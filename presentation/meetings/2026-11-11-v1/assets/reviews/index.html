How this review was done

Third review (V3), run overnight on 11-12 November 2026 (22:56 to 05:27 CST) on the current files: the HDMI Extension Board V37, the Frontend 14 layers V3, FPGA-LDO Option A V12 and Option B V2, and the ASIC boards top and bottom V4. 128 AI agents (Claude) took part. Twenty topic reviewers and status auditors started from the open list of the 11 November review (V2: the first review plus the evening re-check) and its not-checked items. Their findings were merged and compared with that list; every new blocker and major was re-checked by a skeptic who tried to refute it, with a tiebreak when it was refuted; minor items were checked in batches; a critic per board named what was still unchecked, and focused reviewers checked it; per-board writers sorted the items into categories and wrote the action plan. Before publishing, a second independent check re-examined every new blocker and major, every closure and every severity raise (55 items: 25 upheld as stated, 30 corrected, 0 re-rated, 0 not upheld), and two more agents looked for the same problem listed under two ids.

Nothing has been built, powered or measured. "Needs a measurement" and "needs a vendor answer" items cannot be settled from files. A clean KiCad check means only that KiCad's rules pass. This review changed no design file. It is not fabrication approval.

Paths such as boards/…, exports/…, inputs/… and known/… name files in the review's working copy: the same board files as the downloads on the board pages, KiCad exports made from them, and the earlier review lists (published as design review V2). The working copy is not published separately.
