What stops ordering

Pin map V6 is not drawn. The Frontend, FPGA-LDO A V12 and the Option B router still follow V5, so 6 far-chip outputs per port (12 per stack) reach no FPGA pin and the returned clocks land on balls that are not clock-capable. The far chips' SPI, latch and clock inputs already reach FPGA balls and need only a new pin file. As laid out, FPGA-LDO A V12 has room for only 3-4 of the 12 new lines.

Next step: Ask the ASIC designer the pin map questions. Decide Option B and how the spare contacts are used. Then freeze V6 and draw it once: FPGA-LDO A V13 with all 120 signals re-planned (LDO_A_V11/scripts/plan_v11.py), the Frontend's 12 new routes and 4 clock swaps, and one new pin file. Trace: pin map V6 trace (CSV).

A-P, AB-01, SYS-53, SYS-59, SYS-64, SYS-65, SYS-73, SYS-82, LDOA-17, R2S-09

The ASIC boards V4 cannot be made. 20 (top) / 22 (bottom) blind vias run from F.Cu down to In2-In8, deeper than any listed process. All 119 F.Cu-to-In1 laser vias on each board (0.15 mm hole, 0.25 mm pad) break the published laser-via rules. Tracks, via rings and hole-to-plane gaps are below standard limits, and the KiCad rules are set to the drawn values, so DRC cannot flag them.

Next step: Re-route the B.Cu control bus between the dies so every deep via becomes a through via. Give the 111 laser vias that have room 0.30 mm pads and move the other 11. Get the chosen fabricator's written yes and put its numbers in a .kicad_dru (https://jlcpcb.com/help/article/hdi-pcb-capabilities-faq, https://www.pcbway.com/hdi-pcb.html). Via counts: exports/asic_top/vias.csv and exports/asic_bottom/vias.csv.

A-0, AB-02, AB-03, AB-05, A-4

The ASIC boards V4 have no bonding package. There is no die outline, die centre or orientation mark, and no V4 bond diagram. Wire, die attach, finish and encapsulation are not specified, and none of the 30 capacitors has a value.

Next step: Send the ASIC designer and the bonding house their questions this week. When the answers arrive, draw the die outline on U01, issue one bonding drawing per board type and enter the capacitor values.

AB-12, AB-10, AB-11, AB-13, AB-14, AB-15, AB-18, AB-33, A-3, A-5, ASIC-03

Stack heights are not closed. The DF40 mated height can be as low as 1.35 mm. At that height the upper ASIC board's capacitors leave only 1.00 mm above the lower dies. The two stacked J1 pins can hold a joint open. The Frontend's C4/C42/C59 can touch FPGA-LDO A's ±12 V copper. ASIC board thickness is still 1.6 mm in the files and 0.8 mm in the fit records.

Next step: Decide the thickness and the C4/C42/C59 fix (see decisions). Fit J1 on the near board only, with a real part that has a short tail. Use 1.35 mm in every DF40 clearance check (https://www.hirose.com/en/product/series/DF40). Rebuild the fit and print models.

SYS-06, SYS-54, LDOB-35, LDOB-41, LDOB-55, LDOB-01

How are the last spare Frontend-to-FPGA-LDO contacts used in pin map V6?

Options: After V6, 3 of the 15 spare J1/J2 contacts remain, or 7 if the top chips may share SPI clock and latch. They could carry:
- rail enable plus power-good (2 contacts);
- DISC_OUT from the four near chips (4);
- a separate STIM_EN per port (1-2).
J3/J4 pins 2-8, 24 and 58 can no longer become grounds, because they carry V6 outputs.

Recommendation: Wait for the ASIC designer's answers.
- If the designer gives any power order or powered-off limit, reserve enable and power-good first. Rewire PGFB so that power-good reports the rails, not the inputs.
- Add DISC_OUT only if it must be read.
- If DISC_OUT may be grounded, ground J3/J4 pins 3/5 for extra returns.
- Drop the old plans to make the spare pins ground.

SYS-63, SYS-10, R2S-07, AB-22, AB-28, SYS-60, R2S-05, LDOB-04, LDOA-15, LDOA-41

How may FPGA-LDO A V13 make room for the V6 lines?

Options: (a) The only complete route found so far, a grid route with about 45 laser-on-buried via pairs and 50-80 mm of new L2 pockets in the AGND plane under the connector wings. As found it fails KiCad DRC, puts 37 laser vias too close to same-net buried vias for JLC and cuts 62 mm² from L2 AGND, so it is not usable as is. (b) First try a route by hand that uses pockets only. (c) Route 8 new lines instead of 12, if the top chips may share SPI clock and latch.

Recommendation: Ask JLC now whether a laser via may sit on a capped buried via in stack JLCH061611Y1-1078. Try (c) and (b) first. Accept (a) only with JLC's written yes and no pocket under a CLK32 lane.

SYS-59, SYS-53, AB-28, LDOA-54

Which ground plan for FPGA-LDO A V13?

Options: (a) One ground: merge AGND into GND and delete NT1. This was the 11 Nov fix plan's choice; it reverses the 4 Nov single-tie choice. (b) Keep two grounds: GND under the lane and CLK32 paths, AGND islands under the regulators, NT1 as the only tie, and each regulator on the ground of the rail it feeds. (c) Keep V12 and measure.

Recommendation: (a), unless the ASIC designer needs AGND kept separate all the way to the regulators; in that case (b). Decide before any V13 copper. This one choice also settles LDOA-10, -11, -12 and -16.

LDOA-02, LDOA-10, LDOA-11, LDOA-12, LDOA-16, LDOA-54, LDOA-40, AB-17

Options: (a) 1.6 mm, as in both files: the stack is about 17 mm tall, the probe rows are 3.1 / 6.2 / 3.1 mm apart, and the board stays flatter.
