Decisions for the owner

Where may the break-off tabs go on the Frontend and FPGA-LDO A panels?

Options: (a) Keep panel V1 and ask JLC to cut and file the tabs flush. (b) Move the Frontend's two south tabs and the tabs beside J1-J4, C701, C17 and C1 to free edge stretches.

Recommendation: (b) for the Frontend's south tabs, which face the near ASIC board 0.567 mm away; (a) is acceptable elsewhere if JLC confirms it.

SYS-57, SYS-71, FE-07, LDOA-38

How is each head held, keyed and handled?

Options: (a) A printed housing or edge clamp. It seats each ASIC stack fully on both connectors, carries FPGA-LDO A, uses the ASIC board's uneven outline as a key, lifts the Frontend straight off, and covers the exposed DF40s. Add a rule to mate and unmate only with power off. (b) Board markings and a written fit procedure only.

Recommendation: Set aside by the owner on 13 Nov 2026. Decide before eight heads are built; one bench stack can be handled by hand with care. (a), plus:
- Mark NEAR/FAR and V4 on both faces of each ASIC board (not TOP/BOTTOM).
- Fit J1 on the near board only.
- Use dummy DF40 receptacles as covers.
- Adopt the rule 'cable off, wait 10 s, then mate or unmate'.

SYS-07, SYS-09, AB-09, AB-25, AB-07, SYS-55, SYS-72, SYS-66, SYS-22, LDOA-43, LDOA-44, LDOA-57, R2S-10

What GT line rate and data framing?

Options: (a) 2.5 Gb/s, 8b/10b coding, 12-bit samples packed. (b) 3.125 Gb/s with raw frames: more cable loss. (c) 2.0 Gb/s: no margin left.

Recommendation: (a), as in the 11 Nov fix plan (the 11 Nov evening fix plan (not published), item SYS-01). Prove it on the real cable with IBERT. Specify a certified High Speed Micro-HDMI D-D cable of 1.8 m or less.

SYS-01, FE-09, SYS-12, SYS-33

What is the rule for grounding the rig and isolating the animal?

Options: (a) Not isolated: an earthed host and one earth point. The stacks' GND is joined at the head by a short, thick link to new GND strap pads, and the animal is tied to AGND at one point only. (b) Isolated: an optical Thunderbolt link and an isolated 12 V supply.

Recommendation: (a) for the first experiments. Add the strap pad on FPGA-LDO A V13, and run the saline and leakage checks before building eight stacks. Move to (b) if leakage fails.

SYS-56, SYS-11, SYS-78

Order HDMI V37 as it is, or make a V38 first?

Options: (a) A V38 with BOM and silkscreen changes only (C503/C504, C704-C707, the LED legend, the switch marks, the labels). Record the FE_5V over-voltage and J4 input risks as accepted, with a first-power rule. (b) Also add an FE_5V over-voltage cut-off and a J4 fuse and reverse protection now; this changes the layout. (c) Order V37 unchanged.

Recommendation: (a). Put the over-voltage cut-off in the next layout revision; a TVS diode alone cannot absorb U700's 8-10 A.

HDMI-43, HDMI-44, HDMI-45, HDMI-46, HDMI-56, HDMI-47, HDMI-02, SYS-67

How does the XEM8310 stay cool, and how does it boot in enclosure V3?

Options: Heat: (a) fit the FANSINK-05 heat sink and cut the lid or add a fan opening; (b) no heat sink. Boot: (a) a Tandem PROM build, so PCIe starts within 100 ms; (b) a standard build with a fixed power-up order, Thunderbolt cable plugged in last.

Recommendation: Fit the heat sink (measure its height first) and test the closed box. For bring-up, use the standard build with the fixed order. Move to Tandem only if the Mac must find the card at power-up (https://docs.opalkelly.com/xem8310/flash-memory/).

AB-09, AB-44

First measurements

HDMI board alone, no cables. Unpowered: R711-R718 read 36-39 kΩ at U701-U708 pin 5. Powered:
- FE_5V at TP700 reads 4.9-5.15 V, and TP701-TP708 read the same.
- PORT_ENABLE is about 5 V.
- Each port limits at 0.64-0.76 A into about 3.3 Ω.
Then set the XEM's VIO1 to 1.8 V and power-cycle. Keep SW601 at OFF until the FT232H EEPROM is programmed, and confirm SW601's direction with a meter.

One open feedback resistor can push FE_5V to about 12 V into every head. A bad R711-R718 removes a port's current limit. VIO1 ships at 1.0 V (https://docs.opalkelly.com/xem8310/device-settings/).

HDMI-47, HDMI-58, SYS-67, HDMI-25, HDMI-50, HDMI-05, HDMI-45

PCIe in enclosure V3: power up once with the Thunderbolt cable already plugged in, and once with it plugged in after 12 V is on. Scope TP14 and TP13 against TP710, and note whether the Mac lists the card each time.

The XEM8310 takes 0.26-0.49 s to load from flash, longer than the PCIe 100 ms limit.

HDMI-49, HDMI-48

Each Frontend alone on a checked port:
- J3.19 to J3.17 and to J3.18 reads above 1 MΩ.
- Set the bench supply's current limit to about 0.8 A and switch it on before connecting it to TP14.
- Check the rails, JTAG at 5 MHz or less, and flash programming. Program the flash before any bonded ASIC board is mated.
- CLK25_PL high level is about 1.4-1.5 V.
- Read the XADC temperature under load.
- If C4/C59 are not fitted, run a 0-1 A load step on VCCINT and VCCAUX.

The 5 V pad is 0.15 mm from TCK. A bench supply connected live can ring VIN_5V to 7.5-8 V. The load step and temperature check the capacitor change and the FPGA grade choice.

SYS-68, SYS-79, FE-19, HDMI-06, SYS-62, FE-56, FE-03, FE-01, FE-47, SYS-61

Bare and assembled boards:
- Bow across every DF40 site, on a surface plate (Hirose allows 0.02 mm).
- J1 tail length.
- Mated gap under the Frontend's C4/C42/C59, with feeler gauges.
- ASIC board marking and orientation, and the rails at the fingers before bonding.

Bow, pin tails and tall parts can hold DF40 joints open.

AB-31, FE-53, SYS-70, FE-59, AB-07, FE-04, SYS-08, SYS-61

Frontend plus FPGA-LDO A, without ASIC boards:
- Check the SET resistors before power.
- The nine ASIC rails: start order and ramp rate.
- ±8 V polarity during a light-load power-down.
- ±8 V discharge time after the cable is pulled.
- LT3582 ripple and its frequency against load, at the J1/J2 ground pins, at U3 and across NT1.

Power-down can take the ±8 V regulators past their absolute maximum. Converter ripple lands on AGND and on three rails. An open SET resistor drives a rail to 3.2 V or ±11.7 V.

LDOA-08, LDOA-09, LDOA-28, LDOA-10, LDOA-11, LDOA-26, LDOA-33, LDOA-51, SYS-50, SYS-16, LDOA-13

One full stack on the real cable:
- Port 5 V current from cable insertion to 100 ms, and through full stimulation.
- TP14 and the port FAULT pin at power-up.
- Pin-19 loop resistance and temperature.

The high-case load is only 0.02-0.10 A under the 0.637 A minimum port limit, and pin 19 is rated 0.3-0.8 A.

FE-02, SYS-02, HDMI-01, HDMI-53, SYS-80, HDMI-17, HDMI-20, SYS-25

GT link: IBERT PRBS31 at the chosen rate, worst port first. Pass: zero errors for at least 400 s and an open eye scan. Then run an eight-ASIC framed data test.

The only recorded line rate carries one ASIC, and the cable pairs are 88-93 Ω instead of 100 Ω.

SYS-01, FE-22, FE-09

Head signals with all chips streaming:
- CLK32 at the FPGA.
- STIM_START and STIM_EN at the ASIC.
- ASIC8_READ at Frontend J1.
- Clock overshoot at the FPGA and ASIC pins.
- Jitter of the returned clocks.

When 10-14 outputs switch together, CLK32 can cross the logic threshold twice, and the quiet STIM lines can see 0.6-1.1 V glitches.

LDOA-41, LDOA-42, SYS-75, SYS-40, SYS-52, R2S-05

Head noise:
- REF_VCM, REF_VDD and VDD_DRIVER noise with readout running and during stimulation.
- AGND-DGND noise and ground bounce at NT1 and at the far board.

The In4 lines couple about 6-10 pF into REF_VDD and VDD10/VDD_DRIVER. The ±8 V planes face REF_VCM and DVDD. AGND and DGND never meet on the ASIC boards.

AB-16, AB-24, AB-17, R2S-05, LDOA-10

Heat:
- Closed enclosure V3 at full port load, logging box air, the XEM8310 system monitor (pass at 70 °C or below), SW600 (pass at 60 °C or below), U700 and C704-C707.
- Head stack surface temperature in still air.

Air inside the closed box may run 15-49 °C above the room, and each head stack makes about 1.45 W.

HDMI-41, HDMI-52, SYS-58, HDMI-03, HDMI-19, HDMI-46, SYS-03

Rig safety in saline:
- Two powered stacks joined at the head: measure the lead current and the step it puts on the recording.
- With the full rig, measure leakage from J1 to earth.

Joined stacks can push 10-90 mA of supply return through ASIC AGND, which is the recording reference.
