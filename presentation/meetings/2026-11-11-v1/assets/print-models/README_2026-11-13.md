- **What changed from set V2:** the ASIC boards are now 0.8 mm in the files themselves (not drawn thinner). Their side pin J1 is not fitted, so the 0.2 mm side-pin clash of set V2 is gone. The DF40 connectors on the ASIC board use the Hirose vendor models. The Frontend's C4 and C59 are left out.
- **Overlaps left in the stacks:**
  - The Hirose DF40C-80DS body sits 0.03 mm into the board above at the 1.5 mm mated height. This is a model artefact.
  - The plug face is seated 0.02 mm into J3 on purpose, so the stack prints as one solid.
- **Nominal:** 1.5 mm DF40 gaps; a 0.5 mm die box on each ASIC; the plug (11 × 5.5 × 20 mm, 4.5 mm cable stub); boxes for parts without a 3D model. No tolerances.
- **Checks:** every STL is one closed body and every STEP is one solid, with STL and STEP sizes within 0.02 mm (verify.py: ALL OK).
- **Rebuild:** scripts are in `build_scripts_2026-11-13/`. Work folder `~/kicad_work/print_models_2026-11-13`, venv `~/kicad_work/enclosure_V2/venv`.
  1. Export one GLB per board with `kicad-cli pcb export glb --no-board-body --subst-models --no-dnp`.
  2. Run `run_v2.py`, then `assemble_v2.py`, then `verify.py build/`, then `publish_13nov.py DECK`.
