## Method

- Same method as `2026-11-04_FPGA_Work/05_Head_Fit_Print_Models_V1/scripts/build.py`: outline and thickness from each `.kicad_pcb` (Edge.Cuts), plus axis-aligned boxes around the 3D bodies of the parts that set the outer size. Bodies come from a fresh kicad-cli 10.0.6 GLB export of a scratch copy. Parts under 0.55 mm tall or under 0.6 mm wide (unless their footprint is 6 mm² or more) and test points are left out.
- **Option A stack:** FPGA-LDO A V12 at the bottom (z 0 = its bottom face). Frontend turned 180° so its J1/J2 land on the FPGA-LDO J1/J2 (J2 residual 0.000 mm). PCB-5 turned 180° about J3/J4 onto the FPGA-LDO J3 (two boards above) and J4 (two below). Board faces, mm: FPGA-LDO Option A 0.00 to 1.58; Frontend FPGA Board 3.08 to 4.62; ASIC board 1 3.08 to 3.88; ASIC board 2 5.38 to 6.18; ASIC board 3 -2.30 to -1.50; ASIC board 4 -4.60 to -3.80.
- **Option B stack:** arrangement and levels of `23_FPGA_LDO_B_Fit_Test_V1_and_Layouts_V2/fit/fit_b.py`: LDO board at the bottom, router one mated gap above it on J5, Frontend on the LDO board J1/J2, two ASIC boards above the router J3 and two below its J4. Board faces, mm: FPGA-LDO Option B LDO board 0.00 to 1.60; FPGA-LDO Option B router 3.10 to 4.70; Frontend FPGA Board 3.10 to 4.63; ASIC board 1 6.20 to 7.00; ASIC board 2 8.50 to 9.30; ASIC board 3 0.80 to 1.60; ASIC board 4 -1.50 to -0.70.
- **Printable blocks:** the same stacks with the space between each mated pair of boards filled over the area where the two outlines overlap. In Option B the 0.5 mm slot between the LDO board edge and the lower ASIC board is left open.
- Every STL is one closed (watertight) body and every STEP is one valid solid; STL and STEP sizes agree to 0.02 mm. The scratch copy of the viewer served all 57 referenced files with HTTP 200 and the deck's GLTFLoader loaded every GLB at the size listed in `models.json`. The page's WebGL drawing itself was not checked (no WebGL in the test browser).

## Nominal or approximate

- **Connector gaps:** 1.5 mm Hirose DF40C mated height everywhere; no tolerances anywhere in the models.
- **ASIC board:** drawn 0.8 mm thick (the deck's fit profile); the saved PCB-5 file says 1.6 mm, and the 10 Nov fit test for Option B used 1.6 mm (its 16.2 mm height also includes an 8 mm thick plug). The two dies are 0.5 mm boxes on their pad area; J3/J4 are nominal boxes. The side pin J1 is three boxes (body, lead, through-hole tail) sized from the KiCad `PinHeader_1x01_P1.00mm_Horizontal` model, which is a generic part.
- **Micro-HDMI cable plug:** 11 × 5.5 × 20 mm moulding and a 15 mm stub of 4.5 mm cable, centred 1.5 mm above the Frontend top face (as in the fit test). Not from a vendor drawing. The 10 Nov set centred it about 0.3 mm lower.

In the model it enters the board below (FPGA-LDO A, the router, or the next ASIC board) by 0.20 mm and crosses the lower board's own pin. This comes from the 0.8 mm thickness and the generic pin model: at the file's 1.6 mm the tail ends 0.90 mm below the board, and the fit test found 0.55 mm clear to the router and a 0.24 mm pin-to-pin overlap.
