01 / Overview

Three questions for the meeting

  1. Can reset and LATCH be shared? Confirm the proposed 132-pin ASIC interface, or increase it to 139 / 146.
  2. Does the controller link require GTP? The proposal says neither CSG324 candidate provides GTP; resolve the link before choosing the part.
  3. Can the complete design fit and meet timing? Evaluate the 35T first, with the 50T as a conditional upgrade.

Status: proposed choices for discussion, not approved FPGA ball assignments, a purchase authorization or validated hardware.

88 inputs + 44 outputs = 132 signals. Independent chip reset or LATCH gives 139; both give 146. Controller link, boot flash, reference clock and power/ground contacts are excluded.

These totals assume one shared recording clock. If eight recording-clock outputs are required instead, add another seven pins.

Throughput and startup

The PDF uses 16 transmitted bits per sample to obtain 2.048 Gbit/s before packet overhead. It calls for a defined receiver, cable and data format, plus buffer sizing for receiver pauses.

Stimulation startup levels and sequence need ASIC confirmation. The proposal calls for stimulation disabled during boot and an ASIC-approved charge-balancing state; separate amplifier resets do not guarantee freedom from cross-chip artifacts.
