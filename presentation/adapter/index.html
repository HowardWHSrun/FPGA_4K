THREE PROPOSED SIMULTANEOUS LINKS · PARTIAL PCB ROUTE

Three custom FPGA boards into one XEM8310.

R7 schematic · R9 partial PCB

Each custom XC7A25T board has its own 19-contact µHDMI cable. A proposed interposer sits at the MC1/MC2/MC3 joint between the purchased XEM8310 and BRK8310, and gives each cable one four-channel GTY bank. XEM USB is the initial path to the PC. The three-port circuit is mapped; the physical adapter and complete link are still being designed.

Every 19-contact cable

1 is the custom FPGA’s 1.8 V JTAG voltage reference (sense only). 2 TMS, 15 TDI, 17 TCK and 18 TDO serve its JTAG target. 4/7/10/13/16 are grounds. 19 is assigned 12 V in the proposed custom cable; the protection circuit is still unbuilt. Shield bonding is unresolved.

At the XEM / BRK joint

The selected eight conductors per bank go to the cable only; matching BRK contacts are open to avoid high-speed stubs. R9 preserves physical routes across all 80 MC1 plus all 80 MC2 contacts. MC2 1–4 and 5–8 are VDCIN and DGND respectively, but their traces and current capacity remain unqualified. TX1 and control are still open at all three ports; all power/JTAG branch circuits remain unplaced.

This table follows the Opal Kelly XEM8310 pin list and the R7 five-page KiCad schematic. A GTY channel includes independent receive and transmit halves, so the candidate uses channel 0 RX for recording and channel 0 TX for control. GTY clocking, lane alignment and simultaneous transfer are not yet demonstrated.

ENGINEERING STATUS

What this drawing proves, and what remains to check.

Mapped in the KiCad R7 schematic

Three 19-contact ports, MC3 bank 226/225/224 pins, separate JTAG probe paths, isolated matching BRK GTY contacts and MC2 input-power contacts. The logical netlist and ERC checks passed. The R9 PCB physically routes all 160 MC1/MC2 pass-through contacts plus TX0 and reserved TX2 for each cable. TX1, control and branch-power/JTAG circuits remain unimplemented on the PCB.

Needs PCB and assembly verification

Actual mating footprints, orientation, stack height, fansink/bracket clearance, impedance and loss, remaining MC3 high-speed routes, return paths, 12 V protection and load budget, shield/ESD, and Vivado GTY link proof. R9 has 109 unconnected items and 19 schematic-parity warnings. Its physical DRC is clear only under a provisional 0.15 mm study rule; this does not establish a fabricator-approved process or complete the link. The earlier R8 study remains separate.

USB capture still needs a rate test

Three boards stream at once, but the aggregate channel count is still to be confirmed. The actual total sample payload plus framing must fit sustained XEM USB, host and storage performance with an overflow policy.

PCIe is an alternate configuration

The J6 card edge shares GTY banks 224/225 used by two cables here. Keeping the possibility of PCIe later means a different population or adapter mode, not simultaneous PCIe operation in this layout.

The custom µHDMI cable assigns 12 V to contact 19, which is a nonstandard use of an HDMI connector. Do not mate it with ordinary HDMI equipment. Each of the three branches needs an explicit protected source, cable/contact current qualification and a power budget for its entire FPGA, routing and ASIC assembly.
