Assembly references

FPGA V54 and LDO V13 retain their source registration and two direct 60-contact interface. The middle LDO lies between ASIC 3 and 2, with common probe-facing edges exposed. The host has separate interposer→BRK and XEM→interposer seating stages.

Gap readouts are nominal design references. V12 retains the earlier +5 mm export datum; its physical-face convention is documented in the source record. Its 1.8 V JTAG assumption differs from unchanged FPGA V54's 1.5 V bank. The display does not resolve electrical compatibility, populated mating, routing, thermal behavior or animal fit.
