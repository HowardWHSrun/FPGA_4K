Three different paths

Recording data →

ASIC J2 → LDO J1 → routing board → cable → FPGA inputs. Eight data lines, READ and SYNC accompany the returned CLK32MHZ clock.

← Control and clock

FPGA outputs → cable → routing board → LDO J1 → ASIC J2. CLKH clocks the ASIC; configuration, resets and stimulation controls have separate functions.

Power and reference

3V3 into J1.1/2 feeds the LDO. J1.49/50 provide DGND. AGND and the high-rail inputs use separate LDO contacts. They are not added to FPGA GPIO.

Keep the cable compact and give signals a return

The candidate uses 51 contacts: 23 signal conductors, 27 DGND conductors and one unused contact. Signals alternate with DGND; no 3V3 or high-voltage rail is assigned to this signal cable. The legacy Hirose FH26-51S connector fits the provisional 18 × 25 mm envelope but requires a custom staggered-tip FPC. An ordinary uniform FFC is incompatible. The legacy part is obsolete; its current FH26W replacement needs a separate drawing/footprint check. The local exposed copper faces the PCB. The far-end face, contact crossing, opposite endpoint and length remain unselected. Contact assignment does not qualify impedance, crosstalk or a 32 MHz capture path.

The current carrier R6 proposal uses Bank 64 HP GPIO. Its assignments are retained here as a conditional target: returned CLK32MHZ uses MC1.56 / FPGA AD20, a clock-capable input; CLKH uses MC1.52 / AC19. R6 still describes a rigid interposer, so its cable landing remains unresolved. The independent Bank 84 study is unselected. XEM main power is 5–15 V, and its externally supplied VCCO needs vendor sequencing. A nominal 1.5 V ASIC rail alone does not establish compatible GPIO levels.

Contacts that stay outside this mapping

J1.1 / J1.2 · 3V3Regulator input. Separate source, current budget and return qualification required.
J1.47 / J1.48 · VCCIsolated source net; not ASIC DVDD or XEM VCCO.
J19 · 24 contactsNetless in the supplied PCB and absent from its schematic. Held electrically isolated.
AC_IN / DISC_OUTASIC auxiliary pins absent from J1. Electrical type and limits unresolved.
AGND / high railsSeparate source paths. No automatic AGND–DGND bond or fabric connection.
What is needed to connect it

Confirm the ASIC’s guaranteed logic levels, digital reference and safe inactive control states; agree the physical cable and carrier endpoint, reconciling R6’s rigid draft with this cabled board; verify mate numbering and cable face; specify 3V3 source/load and XEM VCCO sequencing; then check routed continuity, return paths and timing. The original LDO’s U9 negative-load behavior and regulator component/rail qualification remain a separate power-board issue. Legacy RTL treats IMP_TEST as an input while the primary ASIC pin workbook classifies it as a control; firmware must reconcile this before operation. The carrier regulator’s 300 mA nameplate is not a verified continuous all-bank current or thermal budget.

The source audit compared 116 external electrical pins with no net mismatch; J19’s 24 contacts remain unassigned. This is connectivity evidence, not a powered test. The original source files are unchanged.
