# µHDMI power and shield routing review

29 September 2026. Read-only review of the preserved R10 adapter; no CAD or circuit choices were changed.

The native source net is an external supply passed from BRK through the interposer. It is **not a 12 V rail generated by the XEM**, and MC3 is not the power entry. The R10 project preserves the R8 logical circuit and its missing physical protection parts.

## What the manufacturer supports

Opal Kelly specifies a 7.5–15 V input range for XEM8310 and accepts power at its barrel jack or mezzanine VDCIN contacts. Thus a 12 V external input is compatible with the module's input specification. The documented connector current capacities are interface maxima, **not a budget of spare power for three remote assemblies**. See [Powering the XEM8310](https://docs.opalkelly.com/xem8310/powering-the-xem8310/).

The BRK's barrel jack and six-pin power connector are supply entry options. Opal Kelly directs using only one power connection at a time. The R10 common VDCIN source consequently needs one designated external input and a shared source/return budget for XEM, fansink, and all three cable loads. See [BRK8310 power connectors](https://docs.opalkelly.com/xem8310/brk8310-breakout-board/).

The Molex Type-D connector specification lists **30 V** and **0.8 A at 25 °C**. It separately defines an all-19-circuit current test at elevated ambient with a 0.3 A minimum requirement. Those are connector-specific conditions; neither specifies the purchased cable conductor rating or approves a chosen branch limit. 12 V × 0.8 A = 9.6 W is only contact arithmetic before derating, cable drop, and conversion losses. See [Molex PS-46765-003, pages 1–2](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/productspecificationpdf/467/46765/PS-46765-003-001.pdf).

## Why a direct route through B1/B2/B3 is insufficient

The protection blocks have no chosen component, current threshold, inrush behavior, fault response, footprint, or ground connection. Drawing copper from MC2 VDCIN directly to contact 19 would bypass the required protection intent. It could produce a connected net while leaving the cable and source without a designed fault-current limit. Native connectivity success would not demonstrate a working or rated power circuit.

The remote 25T review also reports that J4.19 reaches buck inputs without a qualified input protection circuit or completed feed to the routing/ASIC assembly. Completing the interposer branch alone therefore does not complete the full supply path.

## Remaining decisions and recommendations

1. **Power load — next critical fact:** obtain the maximum steady and startup **12 V input current per complete FPGA + routing + ASIC assembly**, or a bounded estimate from its rail loads and input capacitance. This determines whether one Type-D power contact is sufficient and allows branch protection, supply sizing, trace/plane/via sizing, and voltage-drop checks. Do not infer input current from the number or rating of the buck regulators.
2. **Source — retain the documented intent pending the budget:** one designated external 12 V source feeds BRK/XEM and three independently protected cable branches. The purchased 12 V/3 A supply is a candidate; adequate remaining capacity has not been established.
3. **Returns — preserve the user's map:** contacts 4/7/10/13/16 connect to the board's common ground with nearby stitching and continuous reference planes. Contact 16 remains an explicit power/JTAG return. The actual cable construction must determine load-return current distribution; do not count the shell as the sole load return or assume all ground contacts share current equally.
4. **Shield — decide the intended bond:** route all four physical SH pads together locally at each connector and provide a reviewed short bond to the chosen ground/chassis node. A configurable bond footprint can preserve review options, but direct versus component-mediated bonding remains a proposed choice until enclosure and EMI/ESD intent are known. The existing no-connect must be replaced before claiming every cable connection is complete.

No final current, circuit, shield bond, or copper dimensions are selected by this review.
