# 29 September 2026 — dedicated high-speed pair recheck

Howard's requirement: all four differential data pairs on each of the three custom cables use dedicated high-speed transceivers. JTAG, power and ground retain their separate contacts.

## Pair allocation

Directions and TX/RX names below refer to the custom FPGA. The receiver pins on XEM are named RX; the control transmitter on XEM is named TX.

| Cable contacts, positive / negative | Custom XC7A25T | XEM8310 | Port 1 MC3, bank 226 | Port 2 MC3, bank 225 | Port 3 MC3, bank 224 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 / 5 | GTP TX0, H2 / H1 | GTY RX0 | 41 / 43 | 21 / 23 | 1 / 3 |
| 6 / 8 | GTP TX1, F2 / F1 | GTY RX1 | 45 / 47 | 25 / 27 | 5 / 7 |
| 9 / 11 | GTP TX2, D2 / D1 | GTY RX2 | 49 / 51 | 29 / 31 | 9 / 11 |
| 12 / 14 | GTP RX1, A4 / A3 | GTY TX0 | 42 / 44 | 22 / 24 | 2 / 4 |

All twelve differential pairs are dedicated transceiver signals. The custom FPGA uses **GTP**; XEM uses **GTY**. These are twelve unidirectional differential pairs, not twelve independent full-duplex channels: each XEM bank uses RX0/RX1/RX2 and the TX half of channel 0. The physical TX2 pair is high-speed even though the earlier two-recording-lane firmware proposal leaves it reserved. This recheck does not decide how many recording pairs firmware enables.

## Verified and unfinished

- A fresh Opal Kelly pin-list download matches all 24 selected conductors in the native R10 schematic and PCB: GTY receive/transmit direction and P/N polarity are correct. Each copper connected component has exactly the two intended endpoints, and all 24 matching lower-BRK contacts are isolated. The 80 upper-MC3 numbered pad positions match the official BRK import; all 57 Type-D signal-pad positions match the installed KiCad footprint. [Detailed fresh source audit](evidence/XEM_Fresh_Source_Native_Audit.json) · [Audit script](audit_xem_fresh_source.py).
- A fresh native custom-FPGA netlist matches AMD's package records for all eight data conductors, with correct polarity and direction through the existing 100 nF capacitors. [Machine-readable result](Custom_FPGA_GTP_Endpoint_Check.json) · [Fresh netlist](FPGA25T_fresh.net.xml).
- The R10 independent copper audit was rerun: all 24 selected conductors connect to their expected endpoints without branches, and all 160 MC1/MC2 contact paths remain intact. [Audit source and result](../17_BRK8310_Three_Port_Interposer_R10_HDI_Feasibility/validation/INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md).
- Correct endpoints and copper connectivity do **not** establish high-speed signal integrity. R10 still requires a selected fabrication stackup, approved controlled impedance, reference planes and return paths, pair coupling/matching, and cable/link validation. Its current planar P/N differences reach 4.0849 mm, excluding via depth; no approved skew budget is set.
- R10 retains 97 unconnected items and 19 schematic-parity findings. Its provisional HDI rules give zero physical DRC violations; power, JTAG, grounds/protection and other board connections are unfinished. The custom FPGA PCB remains unrouted.
- Matching selected GTY contacts on the lower BRK side are intentionally isolated. PCIe through the BRK is a later alternate configuration.
