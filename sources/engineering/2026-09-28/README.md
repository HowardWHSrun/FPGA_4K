# Proposed 50T micro-HDMI contact maps — 28 September 2026

The [R2 19-contact CSV](J4_Proposed_19_Contacts_R2_5V.csv) is an unchanged copy of Howard's dated `2026-09-28_FPGA_Work/05_Micro_HDMI_FPGA_Board_Plan/J4_Proposed_19_Contacts_R2_5V.csv`. It changes the working proposal for J4.19 to a protected **5 V input**. The [R1 CSV](J4_Proposed_19_Contacts.csv) preserves the earlier 12 V candidate for review history. The [visual page](../../../presentation/fpga/micro-hdmi-19.html) displays R2.

This is a **custom link proposal for the unrouted XC7A50T-CSG325 board**, not the standard HDMI pinout or a verified cable/receiver design. The [28 September meeting summary](../../../presentation/meetings/2026-09-28.html#interfaces) questioned the inherited 12 V label but did not approve 5 V or any final pin map. Its eight proposed high-speed contacts remain unconnected in the latest 50T native schematic. J4.19 still has the native net name `LINK_12V`; the 5 V proposal is a documented mismatch pending schematic, source-current and full-load review. A normal Type-D HDMI connector assigns +5 V to contact 19, but ordinary HDMI equipment cannot power or communicate with this custom wiring.

The source for connector numbering, mating-face geometry and standard Type-D contact names is the [Molex 46765 sales drawing](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/salesdrawingpdf/467/46765/467650301_sd.pdf), especially pages 2 and 4. The manufacturer PDF remains on Molex's site; this repository does not redistribute it. The web illustration is independently drawn and links to the original document.
