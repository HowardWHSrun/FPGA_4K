# HDMI Extension Board V21

This folder holds only the current board and its JLC order files. Open [HDMI_Extension_Board_Study_V21.kicad_pro](HDMI_Extension_Board_Study_V21.kicad_pro) in KiCad.

**Status:** ready to send to JLC with the order note. Nothing has been uploaded, ordered or shipped. This is not an approval: signal integrity, ESD, heat and bracket fit are unproven until boards are tested. V21 has not been independently reviewed.

## Known limits

- **Power:** there is no input fuse or reverse-polarity protection. The worst-case 6.3 A at `J4` exceeds its 5 A rating, so bench testing should measure it. Use a 12 V, 5 A adapter.
- **Slot and bracket:** the card needs an x8 or larger slot and a custom bracket. Neither fit has been tried.
- **Frontend:** the older 3.3 V Frontend V4 must not be plugged into these ports.
