# Compact board size constraint study

The earlier unrouted 50T drawing was 43 × 49 mm. The current **38.5 × 43 mm** outline reduces drawn area from 2107 to 1655.5 mm², or **21.43%**. The updated board carries two 60-contact DF40T footprints, the 50T CSG325, flash and translator, five regulator stages, a GTP reference oscillator and coupling network, micro-HDMI, two LEDs, and twelve bare edge probe pads. H1–H4 are gone.

On the frozen Step09 board, the nearest copper-to-outline bounding margins were about **1.30 mm left** (R8/R10), **0.48 mm right** (J4), **1.66 mm top** (J5/J7), and **0.85 mm bottom** (edge probe pads). KiCad includes the Edge.Cuts stroke in the bounding rectangle; these numbers are geometric measures, not a fabricator clearance signoff. The right edge is already controlled by the micro-HDMI footprint and the bottom by deliberate probe access. A further uniform outline reduction without component moves cannot be assumed.

The present placement has zero physical DRC findings, but it has no tracks or vias. BGA escape, two rigid DF40T mating tolerances, GTP pair corridor, regulator hot loops, full supply returns, impedance stackup and assembly tolerances determine the **smallest routable** board. Gerald's stepped proposal reserves L1/L3/L6 signal escapes while L2/L5/L7 remain ground and L4 power. The 116 FPGA-to-ASIC digital contacts are mapped by BGA ball ring in the [escape study](Layer_By_Layer_Escape_Study.md); 39 contacts still cross the centerline toward the opposite connector side. The [east/west J4 comparison](MicroHDMI_East_West_Placement_Study.md) shows the compact east fit and a route-shorter west candidate that currently collides with the regulator cluster.

**Current conclusion:** 38.5 × 43 mm is the checked placement envelope for this revision, not a demonstrated minimum fabrication size. Shrinking more requires a new placement and representative routing/stackup check, with the current project preserved as a separate checkpoint.
