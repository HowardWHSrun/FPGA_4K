# Compact 50T component disposition — Step 08

**28 September 2026. Read-only audit of the unrouted review board; no CAD changes made for this report.** Evidence is the native KiCad `Step08_Netlist.net` (local source; not included in web package) export and `Step08_DRC.json` (local source; not included in web package). This snapshot has 159 schematic symbols and 159 PCB footprints. Of the symbols, 157 are fitted and two (`Y1`, `R119`) are DNP. Twelve new edge pads are excluded from the native BOM; four older copper test pads (`TP124–TP127`) remain BOM-visible but are fabrication features, yielding **141 buyable component references**. DRC reports zero physical and zero schematic/PCB parity violations. Its 499 listed open items are incomplete; KiCad's native connectivity calculation reports **590 open connections** for this zero-route board. No tracks, vias or top-level copper zones exist.

| Decision | Exact references and native-netlist reason | Required check |
|---|---|---|
| **Remove this disconnected legacy island from the compact revision** | `Y1` pin 3 and `R119` pin 1 are the only nodes on `OSC_32M_RAW`; `R119` pin 2 is the only node on `LEGACY_CLK_32MHZ_DISCONNECTED`. No U1 pin reaches either net. `Y1/R119` are already DNP. `C102` (10 nF) and `C103` (100 nF) connect only between `VCCAUX_1V8` and GND as support for that absent oscillator, yet remain marked fitted. The four footprints occupy the west, front-side area around `(7–13, 17–20)` mm. | Remove all four schematic symbols and PCB footprints together. Export a fresh netlist and rerun ERC/DRC; verify no FPGA or GTP clock pin was removed and no remaining orphan net or 1.8 V bypass is unintentionally lost. A *new*, qualified differential GTP reference oscillator and fabric-clock plan are still required. The proposed GTP `IBUFDS_GTE2` fabric path is described in [`MicroHDMI_GTP_Link_Plan.md`](MicroHDMI_GTP_Link_Plan.md), but is not yet implemented or Vivado-validated. |
| **Retain for the current review board** | `U2/U3/U5` supply core, auxiliary/configuration and 1.5 V I/O; `U7/U4` separately supply GTP 1.0 V and 1.2 V. `L1–L5` are their converter inductors; `FB1/FB2`, `R123`, `C124–C133` serve the GTP rails. `R9/R122` are the regulator-output isolation elements. `U6/U8/U9`, `R100–R103`, `C100/C101` now form the fitted 32-Mbit flash and startup path. `J4`, `J5/J7`, `J6`, `TP124–TP139`, FPGA decoupling and bank bypass remain functional or needed for bring-up. | Verify the exact rail currents, GTP supply noise, converter stability, sequencing, flash timing, and connector current/return paths before any consolidation. The two 60-contact pairs still await mechanical approval. |
| **Study smaller decoupling population, without deleting yet** | [AMD UG483 Table 2-2](https://docs.amd.com/v/u/en-US/ug483_7Series_PCB) for XC7A50T-CSG325 calls for 1×330 µF, 3×4.7 µF, 5×0.47 µF on VCCINT and 1×100 µF, 1×0.47 µF on VCCBRAM. These two FPGA supplies share the current `VCCINT_1V0` net. Current `C22–C27` provide six 4.7 µF (three over the table); `C28–C35` provide eight 0.47 µF (three over); `C36/C37` provide two BRAM 0.47 µF (one over). `C20/C21` meet the separate bulk counts. Auxiliary `C40–C46` have one more 0.47 µF (`C43–C46`, four total versus three) than the table. | Choose the individual parts only after mapping them to U1 power balls and checking regulator output-cap requirements, capacitor bias/ESR/ESL, and the routed power-distribution network. The table is a sizing baseline, not evidence that arbitrary references can be removed. Preserve local 0.47 µF capacitors nearest the relevant BGA supply balls. |
| **Study one shared 1.5 V bulk capacitor** | `C82` and `C92` are both 47 µF on `VCC_ASIC_1V5`. Step 06 correctly moved bank-14 `C51–C56/C92` to that rail. UG483 states one 47/100 µF bulk capacitor can serve up to four VCCO banks on the same voltage; banks 14, 15, 34 and 35 share 1.5 V here. All four banks retain their recommended 2×4.7 µF and 4×0.47 µF local groups. | The two 47 µF parts lie in different PCB locations. Select either or retain both only after placement, DC bias and PDN review; do not drop a bank's six local bypass parts. |
| **Keep `J6` for current bring-up; revisit later** | The 2.54 mm header connects `FPGA_INIT_B` to GND for manual configuration hold. `U9` now adds rail-qualified startup, but the header still provides direct bench control while the new automatic path and GTP sequencing are unproven. Its footprint is about 6.14 × 3.59 mm. | Once automatic startup and brownout behavior are validated, decide whether a small solder jumper/test pad provides sufficient recovery access. Rerun startup and recovery tests, ERC and PCB DRC after any substitution. |

`C11–C14/L3` still carry inherited `power_cfg` metadata although their nets are now the U4 GTP 1.2 V converter; correct these descriptions in the BOM. `VCC_ASIC_1V5` presently powers FPGA I/O banks and does **not** provide an output to the routing board's ASIC LDOs. The board area saved by any removal is a placement opportunity, not a proven smaller routable outline.
