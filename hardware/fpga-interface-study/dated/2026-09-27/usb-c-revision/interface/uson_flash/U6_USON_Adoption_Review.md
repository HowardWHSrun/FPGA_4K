# Compact boot flash and carrier contact revision — 27 September 2026

The current USB-C development design uses **MX25U6432FZBI02**, a 1.8 V, 64 Mbit, 4 × 3 mm USON flash. The previous 128 Mbit WSON remains in revision history. This is a different flash generation and reduced capacity, not a package-only substitution. The eight numbered nets remain unchanged, and boot remains **Master SPI x1**; absent quad-mode FPGA links are not implied by the part capability.

The 67,108,864-bit capacity exceeds the XC7A100T's 30,606,304 configuration bits for a single image. This does not establish a multi-image/fallback layout or programmer support. The selected industrial ZBI02 code, 1.65–2.0 V supply, pin table, factory QE=0 and standard 0Bh fast-read operation are documented in [Macronix PM2643 v1.2](https://www.mxic.com.tw/Lists/Datasheet/Attachments/9209/MX25U6432F,%201.8V,%2064Mb,%20v1.2.pdf), pp6,29,37,88. Default eight-dummy-clock fast read is limited to104 MHz; the133 MHz mode needs a different dummy-clock setting. No boot clock or programming algorithm has been validated here. FPGA configuration capacity is from [AMD UG470 Table1-1](https://docs.amd.com/v/u/en-US/ug470_7Series_Config).

Use the project-local `Macronix_USON8_3x4_Floating_Metal` footprint. Its 0.60 × 0.30 mm oval lands on0.8 mm pitch follow [Macronix AN0159 v4 p2](https://www.macronix.com/Lists/ApplicationNote/Attachments/1997/AN0159V4-%20Recommended%20PCB%20Pad%20Layouts%20for%208-USON%20and%208-WSON%20Packages_2.pdf). No central copper or solder paste is placed on the floating metal. A conservative0.50 × 4.30 mm central strip prohibits component-side copper and through-vias; buried internal copper is not prohibited. The chosen margin and solder process still need assembly review.

A joint header escape study supports the part at B(15.0,3.85),90°. One provisional carrier contact changes: **ASIC3_READ moves from J5.32 to J6.59; FPGA U1.E6 stays unchanged.** J5.32 becomes explicitly unused. Other FPGA/contact assignments are unchanged. The routing board must use this new map; Gerald has not approved it. The earlier micro-HDMI map remains unchanged.

The isolated route proof connects that signal while preserving57 other top-header escapes. It does not demonstrate completion of the merged PCB. Exact before/after fields, candidate hashes and the map delta are recorded in [the adoption report](../../reports/USB_USON_And_Contact_Adoption.json). The current native audit, rather than this placement study, determines remaining opens.
