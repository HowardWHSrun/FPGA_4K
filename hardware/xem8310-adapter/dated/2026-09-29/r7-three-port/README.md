Three proposed µHDMI ports connect separate XC7A25T boards to the XEM8310 MC3
GTY banks 226, 225 and 224. Selected matching BRK8310 GTY contacts are
isolated. The native topology audit and KiCad ERC validate the logical drawing
only. This revision has no footprints, PCB, demonstrated physical fit, protected
12 V circuit, clock/link implementation or fabrication release.
