# Third-party notices

The local symbol and footprint libraries include inherited BRK8305/reference-design data, source-checked manufacturer land patterns, and KiCad-derived standard primitives. Their presence is not a statement that every element was originally designed by this project.

KiCad library material retains its Creative Commons CC-BY-SA 4.0 license and design exception. The preserved notice is [LICENSES/KiCad_Libraries_License.md](LICENSES/KiCad_Libraries_License.md); the official policy is https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/ . This notice applies to the relevant library material and does not assert a blanket license over third-party assets or the complete board design.

All model assignments and embedded-file payloads were removed from the PCB and footprint libraries while preserving non-3D design content. Manufacturer names and part numbers identify the actual components; they do not imply endorsement.
